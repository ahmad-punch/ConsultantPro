## Who this is for

You live abroad. You need to get a first NICOP, renew one, replace a lost one, add a child, or fix an error in your NADRA record. Or you hold a foreign passport and need a POC card.

## What the problem usually is

- **NICOP** is the National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis. It is for Pakistani citizens living abroad.
- **POC** is the Pakistan Origin Card. It is for people of Pakistani origin who hold another nationality.
- Many other services depend on these records. Property transfers, bank accounts, succession certificates and company registration often ask for a valid NICOP or CNIC.

Common problems:

- The card has expired and you cannot find the right route from your country.
- Your name, date of birth or parents' names are different across records.
- A child born abroad is not registered, so the family tree is incomplete.
- Fake websites and agents charge extra or ask for your login details.
- Biometrics must be given in person and the nearest mission is far away.

## Who owns it (the office/forum)

- **NADRA** (National Database and Registration Authority) issues and corrects cards and family records. Its online portal is id.nadra.gov.pk and it has a Pak ID mobile app.
- **Pakistani embassies and consulates** run NADRA facilitation desks in many countries. They take biometrics and documents where needed. Some charge a service fee.
- **Local registrars** issue the birth and death certificates NADRA asks for.
- **Courts** only come in for rare cases, such as a contested name change.

## The pathway

1. **Decide which service you need.** New card, renewal, replacement, modification or child registration.
2. **Gather documents.** Typically: your current or expired card, your passport, a recent photo that meets the rules, and for a child, a birth certificate and parents' cards. Requirements differ by service and mission. Check NADRA's list for your situation.
3. **Check which route applies in your country.** Many services can start on NADRA's online portal or app. Some steps need an in-person visit to a mission. Check your mission's NADRA page.
4. **Apply and pay the official fee.** Pay only through NADRA's official site or your mission. Never pay a stranger's personal account.
5. **Give biometrics if required.** Book the appointment early. Missions can be busy.
6. **Track the application.** Keep your tracking ID.
7. **Receive the card.** Delivery is by courier or collection from the mission, depending on your country.
8. **Check the details on the card.** Errors are easier to fix straight away.

For a correction, such as a spelling change, the documents needed depend on what is wrong. NADRA decides what proof it will accept. Start with the oldest official record that shows the right information.

- **Mission service fees** differ by country.

## Traps to avoid

- **Fake NADRA websites.** Type the official address yourself. Do not click adverts.
- **Sharing your NADRA login, passwords or one-time codes** with an agent.
- **Paying an agent far more than the official fee** to "guarantee" a date.
- **Using a photo that breaks the rules.** It causes a rejection and delay.
- **Letting a card expire** before a property or bank transaction.
- **Ignoring name mismatches** between your passport, NICOP and birth certificate. Fix them before they block a bigger transaction.
- **Giving originals** when certified copies are allowed.
