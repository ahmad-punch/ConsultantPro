## Who this is for

You hold a Pakistani degree, school certificate or other document. A foreign employer, university, immigration office or court asks for it to be attested or apostilled. You are not in Pakistan, or you cannot visit the offices.

## What the problem usually is

A foreign body wants proof that the document is real. The proof comes from a chain of stamps. Each office confirms the one before it. The chain used to need courier packages and visits. HEC is now online, but paid attestation agents still operate. That suggests the self-service route is not simple or trusted enough for everyone ([HEC](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/DAS/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx)).

Typical difficulties:

- You are unsure which offices apply to your document type.
- The destination country wants an apostille. Another wants embassy legalisation.
- The university has not verified your degree yet, or your name is spelt differently.
- Agents charge a large service fee on top of the government fee.
- Parcels get lost or delayed.

## Who owns it (the office/forum)

- **The university or board** that issued the document. It may need to verify it first.
- **HEC (Higher Education Commission)** attests degrees. Its portal is eservices.hec.gov.pk and works online.
- **IBCC (Inter Board Committee of Chairmen)** attests and verifies school and college certificates from Pakistani boards, and issues equivalence for foreign school certificates.
- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)** attests or apostilles the document after HEC or IBCC. Pakistan is a member of the Apostille Convention, which shortens the chain for member countries ([HEC](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/services/students/DAS/Pages/Degree-Attestation.aspx); [MOFA](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/fee-structure)).
- **The embassy of the destination country** in Pakistan, only if the destination is not an Apostille country.
- **Courier companies** used by MOFA for delivery.

## The pathway

1. **Ask the receiving body what it needs.** Get it in writing. Do they want an apostille, embassy legalisation, or only HEC attestation? Which documents? Is a translation required?
2. **Scan your documents.** Original degree, transcripts, CNIC or passport, photographs as listed.
3. **For a degree: apply to HEC online.** Create an account on the HEC portal, upload documents and pay the fee. Follow the instructions on screen. A downloadable electronic certificate is issued when done.
4. **For school certificates: apply to IBCC.** Check IBCC's current process for your board.
5. **Apostille or MOFA attestation.** Apply on the MOFA apostille portal for the stamp that foreign countries accept. Choose a courier for delivery.
6. **Embassy legalisation if needed.** Check the embassy's rules and booking.
7. **Check every stamp** before you send the document on. A wrong name or a missing stamp restarts the chain.
8. **Keep digital copies** of everything.

Documents such as marriage certificates, birth certificates and affidavits follow a related route through the issuing local office and MOFA. The right steps depend on the type. Ask before you start.

## Realistic timelines and costs

Reported government fees (check current figures; fees have changed):

| Step | Reported fee |
|---|---|
| HEC degree attestation | PKR 1,000 normal and PKR 3,000 urgent, with a newer schedule reported at PKR 3,000 per document ([studyabroad.pk](https://www.studyabroad.pk/news-event/2025-hec-degree-attestation-updated-fee-and-process-24070)) |
| MOFA apostille, personal or educational | PKR 3,000 |
| MOFA apostille, legal | PKR 4,500 |
| MOFA apostille, commercial | PKR 12,000 |

MOFA adds courier charges ([MOFA fee page](https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/fee-structure)). Agent service charges are on top and vary.

- **Time.** HEC can be quick when the university has verified your degree. MOFA and courier time varies. We do not have verified averages.
- **IBCC fees and times.** We did not verify these. Check IBCC.
- **Embassy legalisation fees** vary by country.

## Traps to avoid

- **Paying an agent without a written price list** that separates government fees and service charges.
- **Handing over original documents** when a scan or a certified copy is accepted.
- **Skipping the step** the receiving body asked for. An HEC stamp alone may not be enough.
- **Name mismatches.** Fix them with the university first.
- **Leaving it to the last week** before a visa or job deadline.
- **Fake attestation sites** that copy HEC's look. Type the address yourself.
- **Anyone offering a stamp without the process.** A fake stamp can lead to rejection and serious trouble.
