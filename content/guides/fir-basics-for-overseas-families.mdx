## Who this is for

You live abroad. Either something has happened to you or your property in Pakistan and you want to know how an FIR works. Or a family member in Pakistan has been named in an FIR and you want to understand the process.

This guide is information only. It describes how the system generally works. It is not a plan for your case. Police and criminal matters need an independent advocate.

## What the problem usually is

An FIR is a First Information Report. It is the first written record that police make of a crime that they can investigate without a court order. It starts an investigation. It is not a finding of guilt.

Problems families describe:

- Police do not register a genuine complaint, or delay it.
- The complainant lives abroad and does not know whether they must travel.
- A false or exaggerated FIR is filed against a relative, often as pressure in a property or family dispute.
- Families do not understand the stages after an FIR, so they panic or pay the wrong people.

Punjab, Islamabad and the FIA each created cells for overseas complaints, because ordinary channels were not responsive enough ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/09/14/islamabad-police-launches-dedicated-complaint-cell-for-overseas-pakistanis/)).

## Who owns it (the office/forum)

- **The police station** with territorial jurisdiction registers an FIR.
- **Overseas complaint channels.** Punjab Police lists a WhatsApp helpline for overseas Pakistanis and help desks at district police offices ([Punjab Police](https://punjabpolice.punjab.gov.pk/node/11223)). Islamabad has an overseas complaints cell. The FIA has an overseas complaint cell ([FIA](https://www.fia.gov.pk/overseas)). Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa use designated focal persons.
- **FIA cybercrime** deals with cyber offences.
- **The magistrate or Justice of the Peace** can be approached when police refuse to register a complaint.
- **The courts** decide bail, quashing and the trial itself.
- **An independent advocate** is the person who advises on all of this.

## The pathway

**If you want to report a crime**

1. **Write the facts.** Who, what, when, where. Keep dates, documents and messages.
2. **Choose the channel.** Options discussed in general are: an in-person report by a relative or authorised person, an overseas complaint cell, or an online report where the force offers one. Cyber matters go to FIA cybercrime ([GNS Law Associates](https://gnslawassociates.com/criminal-complaints-for-overseas-pakistanis/)).
3. **Give a valid authority.** Overseas complainants often authorise a relative or advocate by a power of attorney executed at a mission. See our power of attorney guide.
4. **Ask for a copy of the FIR** and the FIR number once registered.
5. **If police refuse,** an advocate can explain the route of applying to a Justice of the Peace.
6. **Follow the investigation.** The police investigator collects evidence. Charges go to a court only if the police submit a report.

**If a false FIR is filed against a family member**

An advocate decides the route. In general, people discuss these steps:

1. **Get the FIR copy** and read the exact allegations and sections.
2. **Protective bail.** Where arrest is feared, advocates may ask a court for bail before arrest. Bail after arrest is a separate application.
3. **Cooperate with the investigation** through the advocate. Missing a date makes things worse.
4. **Cancellation.** If the investigation finds the complaint false, the police can ask the magistrate to cancel the case. The complainant can object.
5. **Quashing.** Where an FIR is claimed to be an abuse of process, an advocate may ask the High Court to quash it. This is discretionary and not common.
6. **Counter-remedies.** A person falsely accused may have routes against a false complainant. Ask an advocate.

## Traps to avoid

- **Paying to "make an FIR disappear."** No lawful process works that way. People who offer it are a risk.
- **Paying an "agent" to register an FIR.** An FIR is free to register.
- **Filing an exaggerated FIR** in a family dispute. A false complaint can bring consequences for the complainant too.
- **Ignoring notices** or court dates from abroad. Always have a representative.
- **Talking to the other side without advice** after an FIR. Anything you say can be used.
- **Sharing your documents in public groups.**
- **Hiring someone who guarantees a result.** No one can.
