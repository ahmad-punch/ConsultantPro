## Who this is for

You live abroad and own land, a house or an account in Pakistan. You need someone there to act for you. That could be to sell, manage a rental, file a case, or collect a record. Or you have already signed a power of attorney and you are worried about what the holder is doing.

## What the problem usually is

A power of attorney (POA) is a written authority. You are the principal. The person you choose is the attorney, or agent. The attorney can sign and act for you, within the powers you write down.

POAs are among the most used and most abused documents in cross-border property work ([Pakistan Legal Services](https://pakistanlegalservices.com/power-of-attorney-for-property-sale-in-pakistan/)). Typical problems:

- A relative with a general POA sells the property and keeps the money.
- The attorney acts against your wishes, for example by selling below value.
- The POA stays valid for years after trust has gone.
- The document was rejected in Pakistan because of a drafting or attestation error.

Reporting says the government gave consulates registrar powers to reduce POA fraud ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40395647)).

## Who owns it (the office/forum)

- **Pakistani embassy or consulate** where you live: attests your signature and identity.
- **NADRA and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs** run a digital POA system for overseas Pakistanis at poa.nadra.gov.pk. Some missions have stopped accepting paper POAs by courier, so check your mission's current notice. A Zameen explainer covers the older paper route ([Zameen](https://zameen.com/blog/overseas-pakistanis-heres-how-to-get-power-of-attorney-attested-and-registered.html)).
- **Sub-registrar or relevant registrar** in Pakistan: registers the POA when it is used for property.
- **Civil court or criminal court** if the attorney abuses the power.

## The pathway

**To make a POA**

1. **Decide what you need.** Write down the exact task. "Collect my fard and file a mutation application" is very different from "do anything with my land."
2. **Choose the type.** A special POA covers named acts. A general POA covers wide acts. For property, prefer special and narrow.
3. **Have it drafted.** Use an advocate in Pakistan or a template your mission accepts. Errors get documents rejected. Property POAs are normally on stamp paper and must name the purpose and the property.
4. **Execute it.** For paper POAs you appear in person at the mission with your original passport or NICOP and sign before consular staff. Mail submissions are generally not accepted ([PHC London](https://www.phclondon.org/attestation)). For the digital route, follow the steps on the NADRA POA portal.
5. **Re-attestation, if required.** Some missions ask that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Pakistan also stamp the document, done by the attorney. Ask your mission.
6. **Register in Pakistan.** The attorney registers it with the sub-registrar. One mission states a four month deadline for POAs it attests ([PHC London](https://www.phclondon.org/attestation)). Confirm the deadline for your mission.

**To cancel a POA**

1. **Draft a revocation deed** with an advocate, using the same type of formalities as the original.
2. **Execute and attest it** the same way, at your mission or through the digital route.
3. **Register or lodge it** with the same registrar where the POA was registered.
4. **Notify third parties in writing:** the attorney, banks, the housing society, the revenue office, and any buyer you know about.
5. **Keep proof of every notice.**

Revocation stops the attorney from acting after that point. It does not undo a sale already completed. If a sale has already happened, you need a court remedy.

## Realistic timelines and costs

- **Execution at a mission.** Documents are often returned the same day in person, but requirements and queues differ by country. Mission fees vary. One mission charged 20 euros per document. Check your own.
- **Digital POA.** Timing depends on the system and your documents. We do not have a verified average.
- **Registration in Pakistan.** Depends on the office. We do not have reliable figures.
- **Stamp duty and registration fees** depend on province and the value or type of POA. Ask the registrar or your advocate. We do not quote figures here.
- **If abused.** A court case to cancel a sale and recover property can take years. See our guide on how a civil court case works.

## Traps to avoid

- **A general POA to one relative.** It is the most common root of loss.
- **No end date.** Add an expiry or a limited task.
- **Sale power without conditions.** If you must allow a sale, state a minimum price, name the buyer where possible, and say that money must go to your own bank account, not the attorney's.
- **No visibility.** Check the land record (fard) for your property at least every few months. Ask the attorney for copies of everything signed.
- **One attorney, no check.** Consider naming two attorneys who must act together, or asking an independent person to receive reports.
- **Signing blank or half-filled pages.** Never.
