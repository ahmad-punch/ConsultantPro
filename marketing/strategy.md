# Rasta go-to-market strategy

Owner: content. Status: draft for the 90-day test. Prices here mirror `src/lib/pricing.ts`, which is the single source of truth. Every number below is either an assumption (marked) or has a source link. Nothing here may imply access, influence or shortcuts (decision 0001).

## 1. Positioning

**One line.** Rasta tells you plainly what your problem in Pakistan is, which office owns it, what it will cost, and then stays on the case until it is closed.

**What we are.** A navigation and case-management service for overseas Pakistanis. We write a plain-English Pathway Memo, run errands, track the case, and connect people to independent advocates who give the legal advice and bill the client directly.

**What we are not.** We are not a law firm. We are not a fixer. We do not sell access, speed-ups or outcomes. We follow the official process.

**Why anyone should care.** Government help exists but is split across offices. Punjab, Islamabad and the FIA each set up overseas complaint cells. There are new special courts for overseas property. But nobody tracks one person's case across police, revenue, NADRA, FBR and courts. That job is ours ([plan section 1.5](../docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md)). Most competitors say "contact us for a quote" and publish no payment method. We publish prices and take card payment ([plan section 1.6](../docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md)).

**The promise we can keep.**

- A written answer, a written plan, and a named person.
- Prices on the page before you ask.
- You never pay for more than the next stage.
- Full refund if a Pathway Memo is not delivered in the stated time (assumption, plan section 6.2). No refunds tied to court outcomes.

**The promises we never make.** A result, a date, a shortcut, "guaranteed". If a customer wants the problem to "go away" through influence, we decline. That customer is a no-go signal for the business (plan section 10.2).

**Voice.** Plain English. Short sentences. Calm. No hype. Urdu is written for ordinary readers, not for show. We say what we do not know.

## 3. The trust stack

Trust is the binding constraint, not price. People have seen agents take money and disappear. The FIA has repeatedly warned about fake agents ([The Times Union](https://www.thetimesunion.com/fia-warns-pakistanis-against-illegal-overseas-employment-agents/)). So every touchpoint should carry some part of the stack (plan section 6.2).

| Element | How it shows up in marketing |
|---|---|
| Published prices | Every post and the price sheet show the real price. Competitors quote "on request" and one market comparison calls that "an opaque pricing model" ([Opaige](https://opaige.com/blog/best-visa-agents-pakistan-2026)) |
| Pay per stage | "You never pay for more than the next step." Say it in every first reply |
| Card payment | "Pay by card from the UK, US or Canada." PayPal does not work for Pakistani businesses ([Wise](https://wise.com/pk/blog/paypal-account-in-pakistan)) |
| Video calls | Offer a 15-minute free look at the process on request (TODO: decide whether this is a cost we want) |
| Written memos | Show an anonymised sample memo (TODO: create one from a real case with consent, never invent) |
| Named case manager | Each client gets a name and a face on the first call |
| Low-cost first touch | The $15 question and $49 call do the job that free consults do for other firms ([The Case Lawyer](https://thecaselawyer.com/services/overseas-legal-services/)) |
| Reviews | Collect at case closure. Publish only with written consent. No reviews until we have real ones |
| Refund policy | Stated in plain words on the pricing page |
| Government links | Link to official portals (OPC, OPF, NADRA, HEC). We show we are not hiding the free route |

**Rule.** No fake testimonials, no "trusted by" numbers, no stock photos of a "team". Placeholders say `TODO:`.

## 4. Channels, in order

Order follows plan section 6.1. Do the first before the second. Do not spread thin.

### 4.1 Warm WhatsApp (weeks 1 to 8, never stops)

The goal of the test is to reach 500 or more people in warm channels (assumption). Plan: message 200 warm contacts and post in 5 WhatsApp groups in the first sales weeks.

- Founders message friends and family directly. Not a broadcast. A short personal note, then the link.
- Ask for one thing: "Do you know anyone with a problem back home who would want a straight answer?"
- Record every conversation in the control panel under Channels and Leads.
- Reply within 4 hours in waking hours. Speed is a trust signal.

### 4.2 Community associations

- UK mosques and community centres. Aim for 2 visits in the first sales weeks. Ask to put a printed one-page price sheet on the notice board, not to preach.
- Pakistani professional associations in North America. OPEN reports 350 charter members, 2,000 general members and about 50,000 on its mailing list ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_Pakistani_Entrepreneurs_of_North_America)). Offer a free 20-minute talk: "Five mistakes overseas Pakistanis make with property papers." The speaker is the case manager.
- Ask permission first. Use `group-intro-scripts.md`.

### 4.3 UK community centres

Community centres reach the Mirpuri and Punjabi land families that most need us.

- Offer a drop-in session with a printed price sheet in English and Urdu.
- Bring no promises and no "legal advice". Say "general information".
- Ask the centre manager if they can hand out a flyer. Offer to leave it with them, not push.

### 4.4 US and Canada professional associations

- Target chapters, alumni groups and Pakistani business groups.
- Lead with tax filer status, attestation and property checks. This segment prefers paperwork help to dispute help.
- Offer to publish a short guide in their newsletter. Offer our guides as the content.

### 4.5 Facebook groups

Overseas property groups already exist and circulate complaints and referrals ([Pak Fellow list](https://pakfellow.com/facebook-groups.html)). Member counts need a login to see (unverified).

- Join as a person first. Read the rules.
- Ask the admin before posting anything promotional.
- Add value: answer questions with a link to a guide. Do not drop a sales message.
- Never name a person or a family in a dispute.

### 4.6 YouTube and TikTok explainers

- Presented by a case manager or a retired adviser who has agreed to be named. Not by lawyers.
- Reason: Bar Council Rule 135 bars advocates from soliciting work by advertisement or any other means ([plan section 6.1](../docs/BUSINESS-PLAN.md)). Until a Bar Council member confirms a format, lawyers do not appear on camera for Rasta. This is an open decision in `docs/STATUS.md`.
- Format: 60 to 90 seconds, English with Urdu subtitles, one problem each. See `video-scripts.md`.
- End with the site link and the disclaimer line.

### 4.7 Partnerships

- **UK Pakistani solicitors.** They cannot act in Pakistan. We can route Pakistan-side work to independent advocates. Any partner referral fee flows only on company fees and never on advocate fees (plan section 6.1). TODO: confirm with a Bar Council member before signing anything.
- **Remittance apps and RDA banks.** Offer a co-branded guide. Roshan Digital Account has $13.9B in cumulative inflows and over 966,000 accounts ([24NewsHD](https://www.24newshd.tv/13-Sep-2026/roshan-digital-accounts-cross-966-000-deposits-exceed-dollar-13-9-billion)). Many of those people need property and tax help.
- **Diaspora media.** Pakistan Link, The News London edition, Daily Ausaf. Pitch a column of practical guides, not an advert. Month 2 or later.

## 5. The ambassador programme

**Idea.** Trusted people inside a community introduce Rasta to others. They earn a fee when someone they referred pays.

**Terms (all assumptions).**

- 10 to 15 percent of the referred client's first purchase of a Rasta product. Start at 10 percent. Move to 15 percent for top performers.
- Paid only on Rasta's own fees. Never on any advocate's fee. This keeps us clear of touting rules (plan section 6.1; research 03 section 3).
- Paid after the client has paid and the memo is delivered, so no ambassador is paid on a refunded case.
- Each ambassador gets a referral code. The intake form stores it. The control panel tracks it.
- Target: 3 to 5 ambassadors in weeks 5 to 6 of the test.

**Rules for ambassadors.**

- They introduce. They do not advise, collect money or promise results.
- They never say "we can speed it up" or hint at any shortcut.
- They disclose they receive a fee when asked.

**Who to pick.** Well-known and trusted community members who have no conflict with our partner advocates. Not advocates. TODO: confirm with a Bar Council member that advocates, including any related to the team, may not be ambassadors.

Full pitch: `ambassador-pitch.md`.

## 7. The 90-day metrics and gate

From plan section 10.2. All thresholds are assumptions.

**Go** if, within 60 days of the first sale:

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Paid cases | At least 20 |
| Blended revenue per paid interaction | At least $40 |
| Customers from referrals of earlier customers | At least 30 percent |
| Retainer sign-ups | At least 3 |
| Median time to deliver a Pathway Memo | Under 5 working days |
| Customers who would recommend | At least 8 of 10 |

**No-go** if any of these happen:

- Fewer than 10 paid cases despite reaching 500 or more people in warm channels.
- Customers consistently want the problem to go away through influence or shortcuts. That is a fixer business and we decline it.
- Partner advocates cannot be held to fixed fees and timelines.

**Mixed result.** For example 12 to 19 cases with strong recommendations. Run one more 30-day round with one change only, then decide.

**Where to read it.** The control panel metrics page computes these from cases, payments and channels.

## 8. Weekly operating rhythm

Keep it small. Two founders and a case manager role.

| Day | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Review last week's numbers: leads by source, paid cases, memos due. Pick this week's one post and one video | Both founders |
| Monday | Send this week's WhatsApp post and group posts per calendar | Founder A |
| Tuesday | Intake triage. Every new lead gets a reply within 4 hours and a category in the panel | Case manager |
| Wednesday | Memo drafting block. Memos due this week go to the advocate for a legal-content check | Case manager and advocate |
| Thursday | Ambassador check-in message. Pay ambassadors for confirmed cases | Founder B |
| Thursday | Record or edit one video | Case manager |
| Friday | Deliver memos. Send milestone updates to every open client, even when nothing moved | Case manager |
| Friday | Update channels list: status, last posted, next action | Founder A |
| Friday afternoon | 30-minute review: one thing that worked, one that did not, one change for next week | Both founders |

**Standing rules.**

- Update `docs/STATUS.md` and the channels tracker weekly.
- Log every referral source on the lead.
- Ask every closed customer two questions: "Would you recommend us?" and "Who else could we help?"
- Stop any activity that gets no leads in two weeks.

## 9. What we say and what we do not

**We may say:**

- "We explain your problem in plain English and tell you the next step."
- "Prices are published. See the price sheet."
- "We connect you to an independent advocate. They give the legal advice and bill you directly."
- "This is general information, not legal advice."

**We never say:**

- Anything about shortcuts, influence or special access.
- "We will get your property back."
- "Guaranteed", "fast track", "we can speed it up".
- Any number without a source or an `(assumption)` label.
