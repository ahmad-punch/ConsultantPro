## 1. Independence and status

1.1 The Provider is an independent contractor. The Provider is not an employee, partner, agent or joint venturer of Rasta, and nothing in this agreement creates one of those relationships.

1.2 Rasta is not a law firm. Rasta provides general information, case management, coordination and errands. It does not give legal advice in its own name. Legal advice and representation are given only by independent advocates, who are engaged by the client and are professionally responsible to the client.

1.3 The Provider remains solely responsible for their own professional registration, tax, insurance, professional conduct and compliance with the rules that apply to their profession, including, for advocates, the rules of the Bar Council.

1.4 The Provider decides how to perform the work, subject to the agreed scope, deadline and quality described in each engagement.

## 2. Scope and engagement process

2.1 Rasta may offer the Provider an engagement for a specific case. Each offer states the scope, the deliverables, the agreed fee, the due date (slaDays) and any documents needed.

2.2 The Provider may accept or decline any offer. An engagement exists only when the Provider accepts it in writing (including by message through the channel Rasta designates). Rasta gives no guarantee of volume or continuing work. TODO: counsel to confirm that "in writing" includes a message in the case tracker.

2.3 The client's contact details are shared with the Provider only after the Provider accepts the engagement. Before acceptance, the Provider sees only the case summary needed to decide.

2.4 Each accepted engagement forms part of this agreement. If an engagement letter conflicts with this agreement, the engagement letter applies to scope, fee and due date only, and this agreement applies to everything else.

## 3. Fees

3.1 **Fixed engagement fee.** The fee for each engagement is the fixed fee stated in the engagement. The Provider will not charge more than the agreed fee for the agreed scope. If the work turns out to differ from the scope, the Provider must tell Rasta before proceeding, and any change must be agreed in writing.

3.2 **No other fees from the client.** The Provider will not charge or accept any fee, gift or payment from the client for the engagement outside the arrangement agreed in the engagement, except as set out in clause 3.3.

3.3 **Advocate professional fees.** Where the Provider is an advocate, the advocate's professional fees are billed by the advocate directly to the client, on the advocate's own letterhead, as disclosed in the engagement. These fees are agreed between the advocate and the client. Rasta does not collect them, and does not receive any part of them.

3.4 **No fee splitting.** The Provider will not pay, and Rasta will not ask for or accept, any commission, referral fee, or share of an advocate's professional fees. Rasta's fee is charged to the client separately and covers intake, coordination and tracking only. It is not a payment for referring the client to the advocate and does not vary with the advocate's fee. ## 4. Non-solicitation and no-poaching

4.1 For 12 months after the last engagement the Provider completed for a client through Rasta (the "Protected Period"), the Provider will not:
 (a) approach that client directly to offer or sell services outside Rasta; or
 (b) accept direct instructions from that client for the same matter without first telling Rasta.

4.2 If a client of Rasta contacts the Provider directly, including a repeat client who comes back directly after the engagement ends, the Provider must report it to Rasta promptly, and in any case within 3 working days. TODO: counsel to confirm the reporting period.

4.3 This clause does not stop the Provider from:
 (a) acting for a person who found the Provider independently and has not been a Rasta client; or
 (b) doing anything the client is legally entitled to require, including an advocate's professional duty to a client. If a professional duty and this clause conflict, the professional duty applies and the Provider must tell Rasta that a conflict exists, without disclosing confidential information.

4.4 Rasta will not instruct the Provider to approach another provider's clients, and will not share the Provider's clients with competitors of the Provider in breach of this agreement.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Provider will keep confidential all information about Rasta's clients, cases, documents, pricing and operations that the Provider receives through Rasta ("Confidential Information").

5.2 The Provider will use Confidential Information only to perform the engagement, and will not disclose it to anyone else, except where the law requires it. If disclosure is required by law or by a court or regulator, the Provider will tell Rasta first, unless the law forbids it.

5.3 These duties continue after this agreement ends. TODO: counsel to confirm the duration.

5.4 For advocates, the advocate's duty of professional privilege and confidentiality to the client is not reduced by this agreement.

## 6. Data handling

6.1 Client documents stay in the client's Google Drive folder that Rasta shares with the Provider. The Provider works inside that folder.

6.2 The Provider will not keep copies of client documents, will not forward them by email, messaging or any other means, and will not store them on a personal phone or personal cloud account, except where the engagement requires a local working copy, in which case the Provider will delete it at the end of the engagement.

6.3 At the end of each engagement the Provider will delete all local copies and will confirm deletion to Rasta in writing on request. The Provider's access to the folder may be removed at that time.

6.4 The Provider will use two-factor authentication on any account used to access client folders, and will tell Rasta within 24 hours if a device or account is lost or compromised.

6.5 Rasta may review access logs for client folders. TODO: counsel to review against the data protection and electronic crimes law in force, and to settle the data protection wording and any notice to clients.

## 7. Communication

7.1 All communication with the client about an engagement is conducted through Rasta, or is copied into the case notes in the tracker on the same day.

7.2 The Provider will not delete or alter case notes. Rasta may read the log of communications for quality and compliance purposes.

7.3 Where an advocate's professional duty to the client requires a private communication, the advocate will record that the communication took place without disclosing its privileged content, and will keep the record of the communication in line with professional rules.

## 8. Service levels

8.1 The Provider will deliver each engagement within the number of days stated in the engagement (slaDays) from acceptance, or by the due date stated.

8.2 If the Provider expects to be late, or learns that the work cannot be done as scoped, the Provider will tell Rasta within 24 hours of knowing.

8.3 The Provider will respond to Rasta's messages about an engagement within [TODO: response time, for example 2 working days].

8.4 Repeated lateness or failure to give notice counts toward the Provider's rating and tier.

## 9. Rating and tiers

9.1 After each engagement Rasta rates the Provider from 1 to 5, based on quality, timeliness, fee adherence, communication and client feedback.

9.2 Providers are placed in one of four tiers: probation, bronze, silver and gold. New providers begin on probation.

9.5 A Provider who breaks the fixed fee twice may be removed from the network.

## 10. Conduct

10.1 The Provider will not, in connection with any engagement:
 (a) offer, pay, request or accept a bribe, gift, "speed money" or any improper payment, whether directly or through another person;
 (b) claim, suggest or imply any special influence over, or access to, any official, officer, office or court, or promise any outcome based on one;
 (c) tout, solicit or advertise in a way that breaks the rules that apply to the Provider's profession; or
 (d) mislead any client, office or Rasta about fees, progress or the status of a matter.

10.2 If anyone asks the Provider for an improper payment, the Provider will refuse, stop that step, and tell Rasta.

10.3 The Provider will pay only official government fees, and will keep the receipt for each.

10.4 The Provider will cooperate with any reasonable review or complaint investigation by Rasta.

## 11. Suspension and termination

11.1 Rasta may suspend the Provider immediately, and without notice, if Rasta reasonably believes the Provider has breached clause 4 (non-solicitation) or clause 10 (conduct). During suspension the Provider will complete or hand over any engagement as Rasta directs.

11.2 After investigation, Rasta may terminate for the breach. TODO: counsel to settle the investigation process and any right of reply.

11.3 Either party may otherwise terminate this agreement on 14 days' written notice. The Provider will finish or properly hand over any engagement already accepted unless Rasta says otherwise.

11.4 On termination: clauses 4 (for the Protected Period), 5, 6 and any accrued payment obligations continue. The Provider will delete all local copies of client documents and confirm in writing.

11.5 Termination does not affect any fee already due.
