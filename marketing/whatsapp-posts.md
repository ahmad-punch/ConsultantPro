## Post 3. Inheritance

**English**

A parent has died in Pakistan. The land and bank account are still in their name. You live abroad.

You usually need a succession certificate from NADRA, and it only works when every heir agrees. If one heir objects, the case goes to court. Heirs abroad can give biometrics at a Pakistani mission.

Common mistakes: leaving an heir out, signing a broad power of attorney, and selling before the record is updated.

Send us your question ($15) or book a call ($49). We will tell you the steps and what to ask a lawyer.

## Post 4. Tax filer

**English**

Do you own property, a car or a bank account in Pakistan while living abroad?

Being on the FBR Active Taxpayer List usually means lower withholding tax when you buy or sell. Being off it usually means higher.

One thing to know: reports say the dedicated non-resident return form was missing on IRIS for Tax Year 2026, and no FBR clarification had been issued. So check the current route before you file.

We explain the steps, check your status through official channels, and connect you to a tax practitioner who files for you.
