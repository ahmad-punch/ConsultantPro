### What we look for in everyone
- Does the work themselves and answers messages within the agreed time.
- Quotes the real fee up front and keeps to it.
- Willing to log client communication and keep documents inside the client's Drive folder.

- Anyone who promises results through influence, special access or "speed money".
- Anyone who wants referral commission from advocates' fees, or wants to pay for client introductions.
- Anyone unwilling to be rated.

## 7. FAQ for providers

**Is Rasta a law firm?**
No. Rasta provides general information, case management and errands. Legal advice is given and billed by independent advocates. Advocates are not our employees.

**Advocates: how do I get paid?**
The client pays you directly, on your own letterhead, as disclosed in the engagement. We never collect your fee and never take a share of it.

**Then what does Rasta charge?**
The client pays Rasta separately for intake, coordination and tracking. That fee does not depend on, and is not taken from, your professional fee. There is no fee splitting. TODO: counsel to confirm the wording against the Bar Council rules before launch.

**Does an advocate pay Rasta for clients?**
No. We do not charge advocates for introductions and do not pay anyone for introducing a client to an advocate.

**Errand runners: how are you paid?**
A fixed fee per errand, paid by us, shown before you accept. Government fees are reimbursed at cost on receipts (assumption). TODO: payment schedule and method.

**Can I set my own price for an errand?**
No. The fee is fixed per errand type. If a job turns out different from the brief, tell us before you proceed and we will agree a change in writing.

**What are the tiers?**
Probation, bronze, silver, gold. Everyone starts on probation with a small number of engagements. Moving up depends on ratings, on-time delivery and fee adherence. Full rules: `docs/decisions/0004-partner-network-and-anti-poaching.md` (Bronze from 3 completed engagements, Silver from 10, Gold from 25, with on-time and rating minimums). Rules in the agreement must match.

**How do ratings work?**
After each engagement we rate you 1 to 5. The clients' feedback score and your on-time record feed in. A provider who breaks a fixed fee twice is removed.

**Can I work with the client directly afterwards?**
Not for 12 months after your last engagement with that client. No direct approach, and no accepting direct instructions for the same matter without telling us first. If a past client comes back to you directly, you must tell us. This is in the agreement.

**Why is there a no-poaching rule?**
We spend money and time earning the client's trust. The rule protects that, and it is the reason we can offer you steady work. It also applies to us: we will not ask you to work against another provider's client.

**Where do client documents live?**
In the client's own Google Drive folder. You work inside it. You do not keep copies, forward documents, or store them on a personal phone. Delete any local copy when the engagement ends.

**Who talks to the client?**
Client communication is logged through Rasta or copied into the case notes. You do not need to hide anything; this protects you as much as the client.

**What if I am delayed?**
Tell us within 24 hours. Delay is normal; silence is not.

**What if an official asks for extra money?**
Do not pay it. Stop and tell us. We would rather lose a day than break the rule. We do not pay "speed money" and we do not make promises about outcomes.

**Will I get a guaranteed volume of work?**

## 9. Interview script (20 minutes)

Use the same script for every provider type, with the matching questions from section 8. Take notes in the control panel. Decide within 24 hours.

**0:00 to 2:00. Welcome**
"Thank you for your time. I will explain Rasta briefly, ask about your work, and then you can ask me anything. Rasta is a navigation and case-management service for overseas Pakistanis. We are not a law firm. Legal advice is given and billed by independent advocates."

**2:00 to 5:00. How the network works**
- Say how money flows for their type (section 7).
- Say the tiers and probation: "Everyone starts on probation with a small number of engagements."
- Say what we require: documents in the client's Drive, communication logged, 12-month no-poaching, no bribes.

**5:00 to 12:00. Their work**
Ask 4 to 5 questions from section 8 for their type. Probe for a specific example in every answer: "Tell me about the last time."

**12:00 to 15:00. Red-flag check**
- "What would you do if the client offered you more money to work with them directly, after the engagement?"
- "What would you do if an official said it would take longer unless you paid something?"
- "What would you do if you could not finish by the agreed date?"

Right answers: tell us; decline; tell us within 24 hours. Red flags: dodging the question, claims of special access or influence, asking for commission on fees, asking for guaranteed volume.

**15:00 to 18:00. Their questions**
Answer honestly. If you do not know, say so and write it down. Do not promise volume, income, or anything not in the agreement.

**18:00 to 20:00. Close**
- "The next step is the partner agreement. It is a draft being reviewed by counsel, and you are free to take advice on it before signing."
- "If we proceed, your first engagement will be on probation. We will rate you after each one."
