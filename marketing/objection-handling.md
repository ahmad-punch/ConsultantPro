# Objection handling

Ten objections with honest answers. Rules: agree with what is true, never argue, never promise an outcome, never criticise a lawyer or a person by name. If the person wants a shortcut or a guaranteed result, say so plainly and decline (that is a no-go signal in plan section 10.2). Prices mirror `src/lib/pricing.ts`.

---

## 1. "Why not just use my cousin's lawyer?"

**Honest answer.** A relative's lawyer may be excellent, and you can keep using one. Rasta does a different job. We are not a replacement for a lawyer. We organise the case: which office owns the problem, which documents you need, what it will cost, and what has happened since the last hearing. Many people also want a second, independent view before they trust one person with a large asset. If your cousin's lawyer is also close to the other side of the dispute, that is worth thinking about.

**Short reply.** "Keep your lawyer if you trust them. We can give you a written plan and track the case, so you know what is happening whoever acts. A $15 question is a low-risk way to try us."

## 2. "How do I know you won't run away with my money?"

**Honest answer.** You should ask that. We cannot make you trust us, so we limit what you risk.

- You pay one step at a time. You never prepay more than the next stage.
- Payment is by card through a regular payment link, with a receipt. We never ask for cash or a personal account.
- The first step is $15 or $49.
- We do not hold money for a property purchase. You pay the seller through your bank.
- If we do not deliver a Pathway Memo in the stated time, you get a full refund (assumption, TODO: confirm wording).
- Advocate fees go straight from you to the advocate.

**Short reply.** "Fair question. You only pay for the next step, by card, with a receipt, and a full refund if we miss the memo deadline. We never hold your property money."

## 3. "You are not lawyers. Why should I listen?"

**Honest answer.** We say so openly. We give general information and organise cases. Legal advice comes from an independent advocate whom you retain and who bills you directly. We are useful where lawyers are not: clear prices, plain English, written plans and regular updates.

**Short reply.** "Correct, we are not a law firm. We explain the process and track the case. For legal advice we connect you to an independent advocate."

## 4. "It sounds expensive."

**Honest answer.** Compare it to the alternatives. Pakistani lawyers' first consultations range widely, from about PKR 5,000 to 50,000 in one source ([Saeed Law Firm](https://saeedlawfirm.com/blog/how-much-does-a-lawyer-cost-in-pakistan)). Many firms for overseas clients say "contact us for a quote". Our prices are published. You can start at $15. If you only need one thing done, the Errand starts from $35 plus government fees.

**Short reply.** "You can start with one question for $15. If it does not help, you have lost very little. Prices are all on the sheet."

## 5. "I can do it myself on the government portal."

**Honest answer.** Often you can, and some routes are free. The Overseas Pakistanis Commission, the Citizen's Portal and the OPF complaint cell take complaints at no cost. HEC attestation is online. We will tell you when the free route is enough. People come to us when they are short of time, the process is confusing, or several offices are involved and nobody tracks the whole case.

**Short reply.** "You may well be able to. Here is the link. If you get stuck or want someone to track it, we are here."

## 6. "Can you guarantee the result?"

**Honest answer.** No. Offices and courts decide the outcome, not us or the advocate. Reporting on the new special courts says the 60 or 90 day target "seems more like a suggestion than a deadline" ([Jurist Panel](https://juristpanel.com/2025/04/14/protecting-the-rights-of-overseas-pakistanis-through-special-court-overseas-pakistanis-property-act-2024/)). Anyone who guarantees a result is not telling you the truth. What we promise: a clear written plan, honest timelines, a named case manager and regular updates.

**Short reply.** "No, and be careful of anyone who does. We promise clarity, a plan and updates."

## 7. "Can you make this go away quickly?"

**Honest answer.** We follow the official process. We cannot arrange shortcuts and we do not try. If a person wants the problem to disappear by influence, Rasta is not the right service, and we will say so. If you want the fastest lawful route, we can show you which one it is and what slows each down.

**Short reply.** "We only work through the official process. We can show you the fastest lawful route and what to expect."

## 8. "My family must not find out. Is my information safe?"

**Honest answer.** We talk only to you about your case. We store documents in a private folder and share them only with the advocate or errand runner who needs them, with your agreement. We never discuss your case in groups. Pakistan has no comprehensive data protection law yet ([Friday Times](https://www.thefridaytimes.com/11-Dec-2025/data-privacy-peril-pakistan-s-legal-vacuum-consequences)), so we build to a higher standard on our own. **Short reply.** "We talk only to you. Documents are shared only with the advocate or runner who needs them, with your consent."

## 9. "I do not trust anyone in Pakistan any more."

**Honest answer.** That is understandable, and many people have been cheated, including by relatives. We make the case visible. You get copies of every document and every filing. You can check the official record yourself. You pay stage by stage. We do not hold your property or originals. We cannot remove all risk, but you will see what is happening.

**Short reply.** "That makes sense. So we make everything visible: copies of every paper, official records you can check, and payment step by step."
