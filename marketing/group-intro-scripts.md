# Group intro scripts

Use these to join WhatsApp and Facebook groups the right way. Rules:

1. Ask the admin before you post anything. Never post first.
2. Be a member before you are a seller. Read the group rules and answer questions for a week where you can.
3. One post, one link. No repeats.
4. Never name a person or family in a dispute.
5. No promises, no outcomes, no shortcuts.
6. Replace `{{SITE_URL}}` before sending. Record the group in `channels-template.csv` or the control panel.

---

## 1. Asking the admin for permission

### English

Assalam o alaikum [Admin name],

My name is [Name]. I am part of a small team called Rasta. We help Pakistanis living abroad with paperwork and problems back home: land records, inheritance, NADRA, attestation and tax filer status.

We are not a law firm. We explain the process in plain English and publish our prices.

May I share one short introduction with the group? I will not post again unless members ask questions. If you prefer, I can send you the message first so you can check it.

Thank you for your time.
[Name]

## 2. The intro message (post only after the admin says yes)

### English

Assalam o alaikum everyone. Thank you to [Admin name] for letting me share this.

I am [Name] from Rasta. We help Pakistanis living abroad understand and handle problems back home. For example: land records and occupation, inheritance, power of attorney, NADRA, attestation, FBR filer status and company registration.

What we do:
- Answer a question in writing within 48 hours ($15).
- Explain your situation on a 30-minute call ($49).
- Read your documents and write a plain-English plan ($249).

What we do not do: we are not a law firm and we promise no results. If you need a lawyer, we connect you to an independent one who bills you directly.

Prices are published. You pay one step at a time.

Questions are welcome here or at {{SITE_URL}}/start

## 3. Follow-up (send once, 5 to 7 days later, only to the admin)

### English

Thank you again for letting me share the introduction. A few members have asked questions and I have answered them in the group.

If it helps, I can write a short guide on one topic your members ask about most. For example, how to check a land record, how to get a succession certificate from abroad, or how attestation works. You are welcome to share or edit it.

If you would rather I do not post any more, please tell me and I will stop.

Thank you,
[Name]

### اردو

السلام علیکم [ایڈمن کا نام]،

تعارف شیئر کرنے کی اجازت دینے پر دوبارہ شکریہ۔ کچھ ممبران نے سوال پوچھے اور میں نے گروپ میں جواب دے دیے۔

اگر آپ نہیں چاہتے کہ میں مزید پوسٹ کروں تو بتا دیں، میں رک جاؤں گا۔

شکریہ،

## 4. If the admin says no or does not reply

- Do not post. Do not message again for 30 days.
- Say thank you once.
- Mark the channel as paused in the tracker.

## 5. If a member messages you privately

Reply in the same language they used. Use the canned replies in `faq-responses.md`. Never ask for passwords, ID numbers or payment in a group chat.
