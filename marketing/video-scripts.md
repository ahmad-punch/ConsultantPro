## Video 2. Succession certificate from abroad

**Target:** UK land families, Gulf workers. **Guide:** Succession certificate guide. **Length:** about 85 seconds.

**Hook (0 to 5 s).** On screen: "A parent died in Pakistan. You live abroad. Start here."

**Script (English).**
When a parent dies in Pakistan, the land and bank accounts stay in their name. To change that, you usually start with a succession certificate.

NADRA issues it, but only when every heir agrees. One person starts the application in Pakistan. Heirs abroad can give their biometrics at a Pakistani mission. NADRA then publishes a notice and waits 14 days for objections.

If nobody objects, the certificate can be issued. If even one heir objects, NADRA stops, and the case goes to court.

The certificate is not the end. You still need mutation, which updates the land record.

Three mistakes to avoid. Leaving an heir out. Signing a very broad power of attorney. And selling before the record is updated.

Shares are fixed by Islamic law, and a lawyer should confirm yours.

## Video 3. FBR filer status and the Tax Year 2026 form issue

**Target:** North American professionals. **Guide:** FBR non-resident filer status. **Length:** about 80 seconds.

**Hook (0 to 5 s).** On screen: "Own property in Pakistan? Check your filer status."

**Script (English).**
If you own property, a car or a bank account in Pakistan, your tax filer status matters. Being on the FBR's Active Taxpayer List usually means lower withholding tax when you buy or sell. Being off it usually means higher.

You need an NTN, which is your tax number, and you file a return. Whether you count as non-resident depends on the days you spent in Pakistan and other tests. A tax adviser should apply them to you.

Here is the catch. Reports say that for Tax Year 2026, the dedicated non-resident form was missing on the FBR's IRIS portal, and no clarification had been issued at that time. So check the current route before you file.

Two warnings. Never share your IRIS password and one-time code with an agent you do not know. And do not wait until you sell, because buyers and registrars check the list on the day.

## Video 4. Degree attestation from abroad

**Target:** North American professionals, Gulf workers. **Guide:** Degree and document attestation guide. **Length:** about 75 seconds.

**Hook (0 to 5 s).** On screen: "Need your Pakistani degree attested? Here is the chain."

**Script (English).**
A foreign employer or university asks for your degree to be attested. Here is how the chain usually works.

Step one. Your university may need to verify the degree. Step two. HEC attests it. This is online now. Step three. The Foreign Ministry attests it or puts an apostille on it. Pakistan is an Apostille Convention member, which shortens the chain for member countries. Some countries need one more step at their embassy.

School certificates go through IBCC first.

Before you start, ask the receiving body exactly what it needs, in writing.

Government fees are small. One report lists HEC at 1,000 to 3,000 rupees and a Foreign Ministry apostille at 3,000 rupees for educational documents, plus courier. Agent charges are the part that adds up. Ask for a price list that separates the two.

## Video 5. Power of attorney: the risks

**Target:** all segments. **Guide:** Power of attorney from abroad. **Length:** about 85 seconds.

**Hook (0 to 5 s).** On screen: "One signature can hand over your property. Read this first."

**Script (English).**
A power of attorney lets someone in Pakistan act for you. It is also one of the most abused documents in cross-border property. A relative sells the land and keeps the money. It happens.

How it works. You draft it, ideally with an advocate. You sign it in person at a Pakistani mission, or through the digital route where it is offered. Your attorney then registers it in Pakistan, often within a deadline your mission will tell you.

How to make it safer. Use a special power of attorney for one task, not a general one. Name the property. Set an end date. If a sale is allowed, say the minimum price, and say the money must go to your own bank account. Check the land record every few months.

You can cancel a power of attorney with a revocation deed. But that does not undo a sale that has already happened.
