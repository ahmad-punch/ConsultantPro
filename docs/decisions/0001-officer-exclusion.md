# 0001. Serving officers are excluded from the business

Status: accepted, 2026-09-30.

## Context
A co-founder is a serving police officer and his spouse is a serving civil servant. Government Servants (Conduct) Rules 1964 r.15 and r.16, Police Order 2002 Art. 117, PPC ss.161-165, Prevention of Corruption Act 1947 s.5(1)(d) and NAB Ordinance s.9 (which reaches spouses and relatives) all apply to a serving officer with any stake in a paid business touching police or court matters. Full analysis: `docs/research/03-regulatory-constraints.md`.

## Decision
While serving, the officer and the spouse hold zero equity, take zero pay, have zero role and receive zero mention in any entity, page, post, memo or commit. The product is marketed on process, transparent prices, independent advocates and retired advisers. Police and FIR matters are handled only by independent advocates on their own letterhead; the company does intake, documents, tracking and public information.

## Consequences
- No "our team includes a police officer" copy, ever. Reviewers reject any PR that implies access or connections.
- Advisers shown on the site must be retired officials or practising professionals who have agreed to be named.
- Any future change requires a new record and written legal advice.
