## [Unreleased]
### Added
- Invoices (`/admin/invoices`): invoices, receipts and quotes with `INV-0001` numbering, status filter and totals, new invoice form (case prefill from the case price, line items, tax, template), detail preview, Mark sent, Mark paid (creates a payment, sets the case paid, logs a note), Void, Duplicate, PDF download (`/admin/invoices/[id]/pdf`) and a print view fallback. Client, branding and template are snapshotted at creation. "Create invoice" button on the case page.
- Documents (`/admin/documents`): branding settings with live preview, document templates (create, edit, duplicate, make default, sections and options) with idempotent default templates per type, generated engagement letters and case summaries (`generated_documents`, PDF at `/admin/documents/[id]/pdf`), and an Exports tab.
- PDF rendering with `@react-pdf/renderer` (`src/lib/pdf/document.tsx`, one layout driven by `DocModel` in `src/lib/pdf/model.ts`).
- CSV exports at `/admin/export/[entity]` for cases, leads, clients, payments, invoices, ambassadors, channels, partners and retainers, optional `?from=&to=`; `src/lib/csv.ts` (RFC 4180 quoting, UTF-8 BOM, formula-injection guard). Public status tokens are never exported.
- Help: `docs/help/screens/invoices.md` and `docs/help/screens/documents.md`.
- Archive and unarchive for cases, leads, clients, partners, ambassadors, channels and content items (`archivedAt`); lists hide archived rows unless "Show archived" (`?archived=1`); metrics exclude archived rows; archiving a case writes a case note.
- CSV import for channels and leads (paste or file, server-side zod validation, preview with per-row errors, dedupe by name+platform and by WhatsApp digits). Parser in `src/lib/import/csv-parse.ts`.
- Category-specific questions on `/start`, saved to `leads.details`, shown on the lead page and copied into the case summary on conversion.
- Metrics snapshots: "Save snapshot" on `/admin/metrics` and a History table (`metrics_snapshots`).
- Repo structure, CLAUDE.md, docs skeleton, decision records 0001-0003.
- Next.js app scaffold (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind).
- Foundation: dependencies (Drizzle, Neon, PGlite, zod, next-mdx-remote, react-hook-form, Vercel Analytics, lucide), shadcn/ui kit (button, card, input, textarea, select, badge, table, dialog, sheet, tabs, label, separator, dropdown-menu, field, sonner, skeleton, avatar, tooltip).
- Foundation: `src/lib/site.ts` (brand), `src/lib/pricing.ts` (catalogue and retainer tiers), `src/lib/db/schema.ts` + `enums.ts` (data model), `src/lib/db/index.ts` (Neon or PGlite), initial migration in `drizzle/`, `pnpm db:seed` demo data, `src/lib/auth.ts` + `src/proxy.ts` (admin password and signed cookie), `src/lib/ids.ts`, `src/lib/validation/common.ts`.
- Design tokens: Fraunces display, Inter body, deep green primary, warm paper background, terracotta highlight; `.env.example`.
- Content: 10 pathway guides (`content/guides/*.mdx`) and the marketing pack (`marketing/`: strategy, WhatsApp posts, group intro scripts, ambassador pitch, FAQ replies, objection handling, price sheet, video scripts, email sequence, channels CSV).
- Public site (`src/app/(marketing)/`): home, `/pricing`, `/how-it-works`, `/guides` + `/guides/[slug]` (MDX, table of contents, Urdu summary, related guides), `/start` intake form, `/about`, `/faq`, `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy`, `/legal/disclaimer` (drafts), group `not-found`, `sitemap.xml`, generated Open Graph image, JSON-LD (Organization, FAQPage, Article).
- Marketing components (`src/components/marketing/`): header with mobile sheet nav, footer, wordmark, HTML Pathway Memo mock, offering cards with Stripe or "Pay on invoice" buttons, cited stats with sources, trust stack, FAQ disclosure list, guide cards with Urdu toggle, MDX prose components, visible `TODO:` marker.
- `src/lib/guides.ts` (gray-matter guide loader) and `src/lib/actions/leads-public.ts` (public intake server action: zod, honeypot, in-memory IP rate limit, inserts into `leads`).
- `public/icon.svg` favicon, `src/app/robots.ts` and a root `src/app/not-found.tsx`.
- `docs/DEPLOY.md` (Vercel + Neon free tier), `docs/RUNBOOK.md` (daily and weekly operating rhythm), `.github/pull_request_template.md`.
- Control panel "Rasta Control" (`src/app/(admin)/admin/`): sidebar shell with mobile sheet nav and a global New case / New lead menu; dashboard (next actions due and overdue, memos older than 3 working days, new leads, pipeline by stage, revenue this month, paid cases vs the gate); leads (filters, search, sort, detail with Mark contacted / Mark lost / Reopen / Convert to case); cases (filters, sort, list and `?view=board` pipeline, new case form with list-price defaults, detail with stage stepper, Advance stage, Set stage, Mark memo delivered, Record payment, Close out with NPS and would-recommend, inline edit, activity log and notes); clients; partners; ambassadors (referred cases, commission due, payouts, referral link); channels (cadence, "Due to post" view, Log post); content (grouped by week, status steps, Mark posted); payments; retainers (start, cancel, MRR); metrics (90-day gate cards with pass/fail, leads by source, cases by category, revenue by product by month, channel coverage, date range and presets); settings (config status, Stripe link status, team add/deactivate, "Acting as" for note authorship).
- `src/lib/actions/{leads,cases,clients,partners,ambassadors,channels,content,payments,retainers,team}.ts` server actions (all call `requireAdmin()`, revalidate `/admin`), `src/lib/queries/*` (including `metrics.ts` and `dashboard.ts`), zod schemas in `src/lib/validation/*` with `fields.ts` (null-on-empty helpers for edit forms), and `src/components/admin/*` (ActionForm, field components, FormDialog, FilterBar, status badges, skeletons, empty states).
- `/admin/project` (Project): tabs for Status (counts and the five lists with the Updated date), Open decisions, Decisions (number, title, status badge, date, expandable body), Changelog (Unreleased first, grouped) and Learnings, rendered at request time from `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/LEARNINGS.md` and `docs/decisions/*.md`. The tab is kept in `?tab=` so each one is linkable. Loader `src/lib/project-docs.ts`; components in `src/components/admin/project/`.
- Help content (`docs/help/`): README with the heading contract, what-rasta-is, how-the-business-works, workflows, public-site, glossary, for-agents, and one page per admin screen under `screens/` (dashboard, leads, cases, clients, partners, ambassadors, channels, content, payments, retainers, metrics, settings, project, help).
- Help centre in the panel: `/admin/help` (search with `?q=`, grouped cards with audience badges) and `/admin/help/[...slug]` (left nav, collapsible on mobile, table of contents, prev/next, "Edit this file" line). Loader `src/lib/help.ts` reads `docs/help/**/*.md` at request time; pipe tables render through a small remark plugin (`src/components/admin/help/remark-tables.ts`).
- `HelpButton` (`src/components/admin/help-button.tsx`): question-mark icon that opens a right-hand sheet with one section of `docs/help/screens/<topic>.md`, its "How it connects to other screens" list and a link to the full page; says "not written yet" when the file or heading is missing. `PageHeader` takes `helpTopic` / `helpSection`. Wired into every admin screen header and next to Advance stage, Mark memo delivered, Record payment, Set stage, Close out, the stage stepper, Convert to case, Record payout, Log post, Mark posted, each metrics gate card and Acting as.
- Client status page `/c/[token]` (public, noindex): stage stepper, what happens next, memo, Pay now, Drive folder, last five non-`[internal]` notes, WhatsApp, disclaimer. Lookup in `src/lib/queries/public-case.ts`; stepper in `src/components/marketing/case-status/`.
- "Client status link" row with Copy link on the admin case page.
- Stripe webhook `POST /api/stripe/webhook`: verifies the signature, records `checkout.session.completed` as a paid payment on the case named in `client_reference_id` or `metadata.caseNumber`, marks the case paid, writes a note; idempotent on the session id; 503 when unconfigured. `src/lib/payments/links.ts` adds `client_reference_id` to case payment links.
- Transactional email (`src/lib/email/`, Resend): intake confirmation after the `/start` form and memo-delivered email from Mark memo delivered. Sent after the response; skipped when unconfigured.
- `GET /api/health` returning `{ ok, db }`.
