## 2. Demand evidence

- **40% of overseas Pakistanis report having experienced a property dispute**, and **about 25% of disputes involve fake sales/document forgery**, with illegal occupation/land-grabbing the single most common complaint type. — [pakistanlegalservices.com](https://pakistanlegalservices.com/overseas-pakistani-property-disputes/)
- **Punjab's Overseas Pakistanis Commission (OPC) has received ~35,000 complaints**, of which **~26,000 have been resolved**; special-court judges have been appointed across **37 districts of Punjab**; **PERA (Punjab) has recovered over 1,000 illegally-occupied properties**, and government sources separately claim **8,000+ complaints resolved and Rs 100 billion+ in property recovered from land-grabbers** over a two-year window. — [brecorder.com](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40395647), [tribune.com.pk (via search summary)](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2581682/expat-investors-bedeviled-by-property-fraud)
- Pakistan has legislated **dedicated federal and provincial courts** for this problem twice in two years — the **Special Courts (Overseas Pakistanis Property) Act, 2024** and the **Overseas Pakistanis Property Act, 2025** (creating an Overseas Pakistanis Property Protection Authority, OPPPA) — legislative repetition and a purpose-built regulator are themselves strong demand signals; on paper cases must resolve in 60 days but "the timeframe seems more like a suggestion than a deadline," and favorable judgments don't guarantee eviction of illegal occupants. — [juristpanel.com](https://juristpanel.com/2025/04/14/protecting-the-rights-of-overseas-pakistanis-through-special-court-overseas-pakistanis-property-act-2024/), [tribune.com.pk (via search summary)](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2590283/expats-property-woes)
- **Dedicated police stations/cells for overseas Pakistanis' complaints** have been opened, another structural signal of complaint volume. — [gulfnews.com](https://gulfnews.com/amp/world/asia/pakistan/pakistan-opens-dedicated-police-station-to-tackle-complaints-of-expatriates-on-priority-basis-1.66991099)
- **FIA's Overseas Pakistani Complaint Cell** exists specifically to field fraud complaints (recruitment/immigration fraud, trafficking); one 2024-era bust alone had **50+ victims** come forward against a single fake-employment gang operating ~5 years under multiple shell names. — [fia.gov.pk/overseas](https://www.fia.gov.pk/overseas), [minutemirror.com.pk](https://minutemirror.com.pk/fia-busts-fake-overseas-jobs-gang-arrests-seven-in-karachi-595267/)
- Multiple active **Facebook groups** exist specifically around overseas-Pakistani property (buying/selling, "Real Estate Advisors for Overseas Pakistanis," "Overseas Investors Forum Pakistan") — evidence people organize online around this exact need, though member counts were not retrievable without login. — [facebook.com/groups (various)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2956103451378036/)
- Overseas Pakistanis sent home a **record $38.3B in remittances in FY2024-25**, ~55% from the Middle East, with 700,000–800,000 new Pakistani workers absorbed into Gulf labor markets annually — establishing the size of the base population with a financial/family stake in Pakistan. — [tribune.com.pk](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2607597/remittances-expose-gulf-dependency)
- **Roshan Digital Account (RDA)**, the scheme letting non-resident Pakistanis invest/bank remotely, has crossed **$13.9B in cumulative inflows and 966,000+ accounts** as of September 2026, with **$8.77B utilized locally** (vs. $2.13B repatriated) — direct evidence overseas Pakistanis are actively transacting Pakistan-side finances/property remotely at scale, which is exactly the population with FBR/NADRA/property paperwork needs. — [24newshd.tv](https://www.24newshd.tv/13-Sep-2026/roshan-digital-accounts-cross-966-000-deposits-exceed-dollar-13-9-billion), [propakistani.pk](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/11/roshan-digital-account-investments-to-hit-14-billion-next-month/)

## 4. Trust findings

- **Illegal occupation/land-grabbing is the #1 reported problem (of the 40% who report disputes)**, and **fake sales/forged documents account for ~25%** — meaning distrust of the local system (not just of individual bad actors) is structurally justified by outcome data. — [pakistanlegalservices.com](https://pakistanlegalservices.com/overseas-pakistani-property-disputes/)
- **Power of Attorney abuse is repeatedly named as a top risk**: "Cases have been seen in Pakistan where the Representative goes against the will of the Executant" — i.e., the very instrument overseas Pakistanis are forced to use (since they can't be physically present) is also the main fraud vector. Government's own response was to give **consulates registrar powers and have them issue POA directly**, specifically "to eliminate fraud in power of attorney processes" — an implicit admission that private lawyers/agents were not sufficiently trusted. — [pakistanlegalservices.com](https://pakistanlegalservices.com/power-of-attorney-for-property-sale-in-pakistan/), [brecorder.com](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40395647)
- **FIA has run repeated busts of fake immigration/recruitment agents** (e.g., a gang posing as "Berlin Immigration Solutions" cheating a victim of Rs 1.9M; another gang with 50+ victims operating ~5 years under shell company names) — concrete "agent ran away with the money" pattern, adjacent to (and priming distrust for) property/legal agents too. — [minutemirror.com.pk](https://minutemirror.com.pk/fia-busts-fake-overseas-jobs-gang-arrests-seven-in-karachi-595267/), [thetimesunion.com](https://www.thetimesunion.com/fia-warns-pakistanis-against-illegal-overseas-employment-agents/)
- **Trust-building signals observed in the market:**
  - **Fixed/transparent pricing marketed explicitly as a differentiator**: Opaige's visa-agent comparison states outright that "reputable visa services publish per-tier flat fees. Anyone quoting 'custom price after we review your case' is operating an opaque pricing model" — i.e., a direct competitor calling out the industry's opacity as an anti-trust-signal. — [opaige.com](https://opaige.com/blog/best-visa-agents-pakistan-2026)
  - **Free/low-cost first touch to build trust before monetizing**: Josh and Mak's free 24-hour legal helpline, and The Case Lawyer's free 15-minute intro consult before a flat PKR 5,000/30-min fee — both pattern-match to "build trust cheaply, then charge for depth." — [joshandmakinternational.com](https://joshandmakinternational.com/legal-services-for-british-pakistanis/), [thecaselawyer.com](https://thecaselawyer.com/services/overseas-legal-services/)
  - **Government itself is trying to substitute institutional trust for interpersonal trust**: consulate-issued POA, a One-Window Facilitation Centre in Lahore staffed by NADRA/Khidmat Markaz/Land & Property reps, and dedicated special courts/police cells — all designed because the ambient trust in ad hoc local fixers was too low to rely on. — [brecorder.com](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40395647), [gulfnews.com](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/pakistan/dedicated-police-cells-to-address-overseas-pakistanis-issues-1.64049941)
  - **"No hidden costs" / itemized invoicing language appears as marketing copy** across multiple law firms targeting this exact audience (The Case Lawyer: "one time fee for the services and never ask for additional payments unless agreed initially") — a direct response to the market's known fear of surprise/escalating charges. — [thecaselawyer.com](https://thecaselawyer.com/services/overseas-legal-services/)
- **Unverified**: We could not find a rigorous survey directly asking overseas Pakistanis "why don't you trust local lawyers/agents" (i.e., primary research). The above is inferred from (a) fraud outcome statistics, (b) government's compensating institutional design, and (c) competitors' own marketing language calling out opacity/fraud risk — all indirect but consistent evidence.

## 5. Suggested price ladder (with reasoning)

Reasoning basis: (1) Pakistan-domestic remote-expert products (oladoc, Befiler, HEC/MOFA agents) price routine paid interactions at **$3–50**; (2) India's NRI-specific comparable (NRI Legal Care) validates a **$60–300/month retainer** structure for exactly this kind of diaspora concierge bundle; (3) Western/global generalist platforms (JustAnswer, Clarity.fm) show diaspora-adjacent, English-speaking, higher-income customers (UK/US/Canada/Gulf professionals) will pay **$20–100 per single interaction** when the alternative is navigating an opaque bureaucracy alone; (4) actual Pakistani lawyer fees for real case work range **PKR 5,000–200,000+ ($18–700+)**, so a concierge service sitting between "free WhatsApp advice" and "full litigation retainer" has room to price a middle tier profitably; (5) trust is the binding constraint, not price — so early tiers should be priced to reduce risk/friction (free or near-free entry point) rather than to maximize margin.

| Tier | Suggested price | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Single question** (async, text/voice, generalist triage — "can this be done, what does it cost, what do I need") | **$9–15 flat** (~PKR 2,500–4,200) | Anchored below JustAnswer's effective per-answer economics ($10-100) and above a "free" tier that would signal low quality; matches oladoc's PKR 1,000–3,500 impulse-buy price point for a single remote-expert touch, adjusted upward for legal/bureaucratic complexity and USD billing to a wealthier customer base. Should feel like an impulse purchase, not a commitment — critical given the trust deficit. |
| **30-min expert call** (video/phone with a vetted lawyer/CA/fixer, live Q&A) | **$40–60** (~PKR 11,000–17,000) | Sits between Clarity.fm's low end (~$60/hr = $30/30min) and Pakistani lawyers' PKR 5,000–50,000 consult range, but priced in USD to reflect that the buyer is in the UK/US/Gulf, not Pakistan. Comparable to Josh & Mak/The Case Lawyer's paid-consult model but with an explicit, published flat price (a trust signal per Opaige's own competitive positioning) instead of "contact us for a quote." |

**Key overall observations:**
- Every routine remote-paperwork product Pakistanis already pay for (doctor consults, tax filing, attestation, visa consultancy) clusters in the **$3–50 single-transaction** range domestically — but the *same* services marketed explicitly to the UK/US/Gulf diaspora (Opaige, international-facing law firms) command **3–10x that in USD terms**, because the buyer's reference price is Western consulting/legal rates, not Pakistani domestic rates. This is the core pricing opportunity.
- The **NRI Legal Care monthly-retainer comparable is the single strongest pricing proof point** in this research, since it is (a) the same "diaspora + property + legal + trust" problem, (b) already monetized at scale, and (c) in a neighboring, culturally/legally similar market.
- Price transparency itself is a monetizable trust feature, not just an ethical nicety — competitors are already using "we publish flat fees, they don't" as a sales pitch.
