## 0. One-page summary

**Verdict.** Yes, this is a real business. It is a good small business, not a venture-scale business by default.

- The gap is real. No player found in Pakistan combines fixed prices, card payment from abroad, case tracking, and coverage of the whole workflow (police, land, inheritance, NADRA, FBR, courts) ([research file 02, section 3](../research/02-pakistan-landscape.md), reviewing firms such as [Josh and Mak](https://joshandmakinternational.com/legal-services-for-british-pakistanis/) and [Interalegem](https://interalegem.org/overseas-legal-services/)).
- Demand is visible. Punjab's Overseas Pakistanis Commission reports about 35,000 complaints received ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40395647)). A law-firm source reports 40% of overseas Pakistanis have had a property dispute ([pakistanlegalservices.com](https://pakistanlegalservices.com/overseas-pakistani-property-disputes/)). Remittances hit a record $41.6B in FY2026 ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/pakistan/saudi-arabia-uae-drive-pakistan-remittances-to-record-416bn-1.500602356)). Roshan Digital Account passed 966,000 accounts ([24NewsHD](https://www.24newshd.tv/13-Sep-2026/roshan-digital-accounts-cross-966-000-deposits-exceed-dollar-13-9-billion)).
- The price band exists in a close comparable. India's NRI Legal Care sells monthly plans from about $60 to $300 ([nrilegalcare.com/pricing](https://nrilegalcare.com/pricing)).

**The model in five lines.**

1. We are a navigation and case-management service. We are not a law firm and not a fixer.
2. A client pays a fixed price for a written Pathway Memo: what the problem is, who owns it, the remedies, timelines, costs, and traps.
3. If they want, we project-manage the case with an independent partner advocate, who bills the client directly.
4. We run government errands (NADRA, attestation, FBR, land-record copies) for a fixed fee plus government fees at cost.
5. Families who want ongoing cover buy a "Family Affairs" retainer at $79 to $249 a month.

**The three risks.**

1. **The serving officers.** This is the largest risk in the plan, not the largest asset. A serving police officer with any stake in a paid business touching police or court matters is exposed under the Conduct Rules, the Police Order, the Penal Code, the Prevention of Corruption Act and the NAB Ordinance (Section 5).
2. **Trust and payment.** Overseas Pakistanis have been defrauded by agents and by relatives holding their Power of Attorney. They will not wire money to an unknown Pakistani company. We solve this with fixed prices, pay-per-stage, card payment through a UK or US entity, and written deliverables.
3. **Supply quality.** The service is only as good as the partner advocate and errand runner on the ground.

**The 90-day test.** Build no product. Sell 20 paid cases by hand through WhatsApp, a booking link and a payment link. Go if, within 60 days of the first sale, we reach 20 paid cases, at least $40 revenue per paid interaction, at least 30% referrals, at least 3 retainers, median memo delivery under 5 working days, and at least 8 of 10 customers who would recommend us (all assumptions). Section 10 has the plan.

**The decision needed today.** Both serving officers (the officer and the spouse) agree in writing to hold zero equity, take zero pay, have zero role and receive zero mention while they serve. The business never trades on rank or "police connections". If this cannot be agreed today, do not start.

## 1. The problem and who has it

### 1.1 The size of the population

About 9 million Pakistanis live abroad, nearly 4 million of them in the Middle East ([Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2330465/overbearing-overseas-pakistanis)). The largest communities are Saudi Arabia (1.81M), the UK (1.66M), the UAE (~1.6M), the US (684k) and Canada (303k) ([Wikipedia, Pakistani diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_diaspora); [Wikipedia, Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistanis_in_Saudi_Arabia)).

They send money home at record levels. FY2026 remittances were $41.6B, with Saudi Arabia $9.78B, UAE $8.81B and UK $6.33B ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/pakistan/saudi-arabia-uae-drive-pakistan-remittances-to-record-416bn-1.500602356); [The News](https://www.thenews.pk/story/1424957-fy26-remittances-hit-416bn-showing-86-yearly-growth)). The Roshan Digital Account has $13.9B in cumulative inflows and 966,000+ accounts, with $8.77B used inside Pakistan ([24NewsHD](https://www.24newshd.tv/13-Sep-2026/roshan-digital-accounts-cross-966-000-deposits-exceed-dollar-13-9-billion)). These people hold assets in Pakistan and deal with its offices remotely.

Research found no survey that breaks down property ownership by country of residence (unverified). RDA figures skew toward the banked diaspora and likely undercount Gulf workers who remit informally.

Implication for launch: the UK segment has the highest-value disputes and the densest community networks. The North American segment has the cleanest paperwork problems and the highest willingness to pay by card. Gulf workers are numerous but price-sensitive. Launch with UK and North America. Serve Gulf customers through low-price errands and questions.

### 1.5 Why government channels do not solve it

Government provision is broad on paper and fragmented in practice.

- **OPC Punjab** has its own Act, portal and app ([Punjab Laws](http://punjablaws.gov.pk/laws/2574.html); [OSPC portal](https://ospc.punjab.gov.pk/)). Its resolution rates are self-reported, not audited, and "resolved" is not defined ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2022/02/28/citizen-portal-resolved-85-of-land-related-complaints-of-overseas-pakistanis-in-2021/)).
- **OPF** runs a free federal complaint cell and warns against impersonators asking for money ([OPF](https://opf.org.pk/service/complaint-cell/)). No resolution statistics were found (unverified).
- **Police cells** differ by province. Punjab has a WhatsApp helpline and DPO help desks ([Punjab Police](https://punjabpolice.punjab.gov.pk/node/11223)). Islamabad has a DSP-led cell ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/09/14/islamabad-police-launches-dedicated-complaint-cell-for-overseas-pakistanis/)). Sindh routes complaints through a focal point and a High Court email channel ([Sindh High Court](https://sindhhighcourt.gov.pk/overseas_pakistanis.php)). KP uses Additional Deputy Commissioners ([GNQ Legal](https://gnqlegal.com/overseas-pakistanis/)).
- **Special courts** (2024-2025) are the most relevant development. They are too new to have outcome data (unverified). Reporting says the 60-day timeframe "seems more like a suggestion than a deadline" ([juristpanel.com](https://juristpanel.com/2025/04/14/protecting-the-rights-of-overseas-pakistanis-through-special-court-overseas-pakistanis-property-act-2024/)).
- **NADRA** handles only undisputed succession ([aiksol360](https://aiksol360.com/guide/inheritance-property-transfer-guide-pakistan/)).
- **FBR** execution went backwards on non-resident filing in TY2026 ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40440429/missing-non-resident-form-overseas-pakistanis-face-tax-filing-issues)).

The core gap: no single system tracks one person's case across police, revenue, NADRA, FBR and courts. A client with a property, inheritance and FIR problem must work several silos alone. Nobody in government is accountable for the whole case. That is the job we sell.

## 2. What the business actually is

### 2.1 Definition

We are a **navigation and case-management service**. We diagnose the problem, write a plan, and track the case to closure. We are not a law firm. We do not give legal opinions in our own name. We are not a fixer. We never promise an outcome through influence.

The scarce asset is **credible diagnosis plus accountability**. Information is cheap; law-firm blogs give it away. What clients cannot get is someone who reads their documents, tells them plainly which office owns the problem and what it will cost, and then stays accountable while it moves.

### 2.2 The case lifecycle

| Stage | What happens | Output | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Intake | Structured web form plus WhatsApp. Who, what, where, which documents, how urgent. | Case file opened in the tracker | Case manager |
| 2. Triage | Classify the category (land, inheritance, POA, tax, NADRA, company, police-related). Identify the forum (OPC, special court, civil court, NADRA, FBR, police cell). Rate severity. | Triage note; recommended product | Case manager, with advocate check for legal matters |
| 3. Pathway Memo | Written plan in plain English (Urdu on request): what the problem is, who owns it, remedies, realistic timelines, costs, traps, next steps. | Signed-off memo; call to walk through it | Case manager drafts; partner advocate reviews legal content |
| 4. Managed Execution (optional) | The client retains a partner advocate directly. We project-manage: documents, scheduling, errands, chasing, reporting. | Engagement letter; milestone plan | Case manager plus advocate plus errand runner |
| 5. Tracked updates | Scheduled updates at each milestone and at fixed intervals, even when nothing has moved. | Update log in client vault | Case manager |
| 6. Closure and review | Closing summary, document handover, feedback score. | Closure note; NPS score | Case manager |

Police and FIR matters follow the same lifecycle with one rule. The independent advocate handles all substance, on the advocate's own letterhead. The company does intake, document preparation, tracking and general public information only. No one at the company contacts police officers about a client's case (Section 5).

### 3.2 What the pattern says

India splits in two. Fintech "NRI super-apps" (SBNRI, Belong, Vance, Zolve, Abound) raise real money because FX spreads and interchange recur ([research file 01, section 1](../research/01-global-comparables.md)). Legal marketplaces and concierges (LawRato, LegalKart, MyAdvo, Beamcrew) stay small. Research found no YC company that is mainly a legal or paperwork concierge for a diaspora, and could not tell whether that means open whitespace or economics investors will not fund (unverified). We read it as a warning: plan to be profitable on our own revenue.

### 3.3 Seven lessons for us

1. **Trust is the product.** Surviving NRI firms lead with fixed prices, upfront quotes, and video or WhatsApp consults ([nrilegalservice.com](https://www.nrilegalservice.com/property-disputes/)).
2. **Collect payment before delivery.** Every surviving marketplace reviewed (JustAnswer, Intro.co, LawRato) does ([research file 01, section 5](../research/01-global-comparables.md)). Property cases drag for months; collections risk would kill a small firm.
3. **Never take a cut of the lawyer's fee.** Avvo's flat-fee product was shut after bar rulings on fee-splitting. UpCounsel's 30% commission failed; the revived model lets lawyers keep 100%. Pakistan's Bar Council canons point the same way (Section 5).
4. **Attach a subscription to transactions.** LegalZoom proves the hybrid at $436M a year. Pure pay-per-question does not build a durable base.
5. **Keep subscriptions honest.** The FTC sued JustAnswer in January 2026 over "nominal fee to join" enrolment into recurring charges ([research file 01, section 3](../research/01-global-comparables.md)). Our retainers must be opt-in, clearly priced and easy to cancel.
6. **Human case work does not scale like software.** Atrium's $75.5M did not beat law-firm cost to serve. Our margins depend on templates, a disciplined scope and case-manager capacity, not on technology.
7. **Ride government digitisation.** Sindh's e-registration for overseas Pakistanis and NADRA's digital POA reduce the manual work we would otherwise absorb ([Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2592014/sindh-launches-digital-property-registration-system-for-overseas-pakistanis); [abdullahcity.com](https://abdullahcity.com/blog/simplifying-the-lives-of-overseas-pakistanis-nadras-digital-power-of-attorney-service)). We sit on top of these systems as the tracking and accountability layer.

### 4.7 Two cost assumptions to check before launch

1. **Payment fees.** The 6% line assumes a UK or US Stripe account. If we use a merchant of record such as Paddle, the published pricing is about 5% plus $0.50 per transaction ([jawadhassan.dev](https://jawadhassan.dev/blog/paddle-vs-lemonsqueezy-pakistan)). On a $15 question that is about 8%. Bundle small items, or push questions into retainers.
2. **How advocate payouts are booked.** The model treats advocate time on calls, assessments and managed cases as a 45% payout from our revenue. Research says legal advice must be "delivered by, invoiced by, and legally attributable to a named, independently retained advocate", not resold inside the company's fee ([research file 03, section 7](../research/03-regulatory-constraints.md); [Bar Council canons](https://www.scribd.com/document/520351703/Chap-Xii-Bar-Councils-Rules-1976-c)). Two workable options: (a) the advocate's share is invoiced to the client by the advocate on the same payment page, so our reported revenue falls but net does not; or (b) the advocate is paid a flat fee for document review and information work, and the memo is framed as information, not a legal opinion. A practising Bar Council member must pick one before launch.

## 5. Legal structure and the officer question

This is the most important section of the plan. Read it before anything else is decided.

### 5.1 The rules that apply to the serving officer

| Rule | What it says | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Government Servants (Conduct) Rules 1964, r.15 | "No Government servant shall, except with the previous sanction of the Government, take part in the promotion, registration or management of any bank or company" ([Conduct Rules PDF](https://paklawyer.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Government-Servants-Conduct-Rules-1964-1.pdf)) | Being a promoter, subscriber or director of record is itself the prohibited act. No passive-shareholder carve-out. |
| Conduct Rules r.16 | No trade, employment or work other than official duties without sanction, except honorary religious, social or charitable work, or occasional literary or artistic work ([same](https://paklawyer.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Government-Servants-Conduct-Rules-1964-1.pdf)) | A paid advisory business needs sanction. For a business touching police matters, sanction would very likely be refused. |
| Conduct Rules r.14(2) | No investment by the servant "or any member of his family" likely to embarrass or influence him ([same](https://paklawyer.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Government-Servants-Conduct-Rules-1964-1.pdf)) | Reaches the spouse's holding too. Rule 3(1)(c) includes the wife in "family". |
| Establishment Division O.M. No.1/20/76-D-IV (1976), applied in FBR circular (28 April 2022) | Government servants barred from private consultancy; FBR threatened action under Civil Servants (E&D) Rules 2020 ([FBR circular](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202243094332733PrivateConsultancyTaxPracticeOfficialsFBR.pdf)) | Real enforcement of the exact pattern: officials advising the public on their own department's business. |
| Police Order 2002, Art.117 | "No Police Officer shall engage in any private employment while he is a member of the police establishment" ([table of contents](https://manzoorlawbook.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/contents-of-Police-Order.-lfwfu6.pdf); [quoted text](https://mcqtimes.com/no-police-officer-shall-engage-in-any-private-employment-while-he-is-a-member-of-the-police-establishment-as-provided-by-article-_________-of-the-police-order-2002/)) | A police-specific bar with no honorary-work exception. |
| PPC s.161 | Accepting any gratification other than legal remuneration as a motive or reward for an official act; up to three years ([PLJ Law Site](http://www.pljlawsite.com/html/ppc161.htm)) | Any client payment linked to the officer's role fits. |
| PPC ss.162-165 | s.163 covers taking money to exercise personal influence with a public servant, even without corrupt means. Wording is a summary, not verbatim (unverified; research could not pull clean text). | "Our co-founder is a serving SP" is close to the s.163 fact pattern. |
| Prevention of Corruption Act 1947, s.5(1)(d) | Criminal misconduct where a public servant, "by otherwise abusing his position", obtains a valuable thing or pecuniary advantage for himself or any other person; up to seven years ([ACE Punjab PDF](https://ace.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/THE_PREVENTION_OF_CORRUPTION_ACT_1947.pdf)) | Needs no single bribe. A company that profits because people believe it has an inside track can be enough. s.4 creates a presumption against the accused once gratification is shown. |
| NAB Ordinance 1999, s.9(a)(iv) and (vi) | Benefit obtained "for his spouse or dependents or any other person" and misuse of authority to benefit "any other person related to him" (summary; verify before relying) ([PLJ Law Site](https://pljlawsite.com/html/nabo9.htm)) | Putting the business in the spouse's name does not remove exposure. The 2022 NAB amendments narrow thresholds but do not obviously protect this pattern (unverified in detail). |
| Civil Servants Act 1973 | Private employment is allowed during leave preparatory to retirement or after retirement, with prior approval if within two years of retirement ([Pakistan Code](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/UY2FqaJw1-apaUY2Fqa-apaUY2NqZpY=-sg-jjjjjjjjjjjjj)) | Confirms that serving officers have no right to outside business. Also governs retired officers we recruit as advisers. |

Current enforcement is active. FIA dismissed a Sub-Inspector for "employing a private individual for official work" on 29 September 2026 ([24NewsHD](https://www.24newshd.tv/29-Sep-2026/fia-officer-dismissed-corruption-use-private-clerk)) and issued 76 punishments in its Islamabad zone ([The Nation](https://www.nation.com.pk/26-Sep-2026/fia-takes-major-disciplinary-action-10-dismissed-8-demoted-among-76-punished)).

Punjab's own commentary on its 1966 rules records concern that "wives of some Government servants have been engaging in trade and business where the husband's influence could possibly be misused" ([SlideShare commentary](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/conduct-rules-1966/68222802); secondary source, exact rule text unverified).

### 5.2 The rules that apply to the lawyer relatives and the company

- **Bar Council Rules 1976, Chapter XII.** Rule 135: an advocate "shall not solicit professional employment by advertisement or by any other means". Rule 136: an advocate shall not employ another person to solicit work. Payment of commission to someone merely for introducing clients is treated as touting ([Scribd transcription](https://www.scribd.com/document/520351703/Chap-Xii-Bar-Councils-Rules-1976-c); [Studocu](https://www.studocu.com/row/document/university-of-sindh/legal-ethics/canons-of-professional-conduct/36459807)). Rule numbers are indicative, not verified against the gazetted text (unverified). Enforcement runs against the advocate, so a badly designed model puts the relatives' licences at risk.
- **Legal Practitioners Act 1879 tout provisions are repealed**, including s.36, by the Advocates (Amendment) Act 2023 ([juris-codex.com](https://juris-codex.com/act_pdfs/Legal_Practitioners_Act_1879.pdf)). The operative law is the 1973 Act and the 1976 canons ([1973 Act](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator4068b39bc9eebedce40baf56a335243d.pdf)).
- **The company cannot sell legal advice.** It can sell information, document preparation, case tracking and concierge work. The advocate advises and bills the client directly. This is how Wukla separates "Ask a Wakil" from templated documents ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1278150); [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/wukla-is-pakistans-first-website-for-online-legal-solutions-and-documents-delivery/)).
- **PECA 2016.** Unauthorised access to data (s.3) and harmful disclosure about a person (s.20, up to 3 years or Rs 1 million) are criminal offences ([Pakistan Code](https://www.pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator6a061efe0ed5bd153fa8b79b8eb4cba7.pdf)). We will hold CNICs, FIR details, land records and family disputes. A leak is a criminal matter.
- **Data protection bill.** Still not law as of September 2026 ([The Friday Times](https://www.thefridaytimes.com/11-Dec-2025/data-privacy-peril-pakistan-s-legal-vacuum-consequences); [Chambers](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/pakistan)). Build to its likely requirements anyway.

### 5.3 The five structures, condensed

| # | Structure | Officer risk | Public-story risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Officer is shareholder, director or promoter, even small or via nominee | High | Severe | Never |
| 2 | "Sleeping partner": informal, undocumented profit share | High, arguably higher than #1 | Severe, and harder to defend | Never. This is the highest-risk option, not the safest |
| 3 | Spouse owns 100%; officer known to be "behind" it | Medium-High | High | Not recommended |
| 4 | Officer is an unpaid informal adviser only; no equity, pay, role or mention | Low-Medium | Medium | Acceptable only with strict discipline |
| 5 | Business excludes police/FIR substance; officer has zero role | Very Low | Low | Lowest risk |

Source: research file 03, section 6 ([Conduct Rules](https://paklawyer.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Government-Servants-Conduct-Rules-1964-1.pdf); [Prevention of Corruption Act](https://ace.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/THE_PREVENTION_OF_CORRUPTION_ACT_1947.pdf)).

The "behind the scenes" idea that started this project is option 2. Removing the paperwork does not remove the conduct the law punishes. It removes the good-faith record that would defend the family if a client complains.

### 5.4 Recommended structure

**Ownership.**

- While serving, the officer and the spouse hold **zero equity, zero pay, zero role and zero mention**, in any entity in the group, Pakistani or foreign.
- No side letters, no promised future equity, no "we will look after you later". A promise of future value is itself an economic interest.
- The tech founder owns the company. Other non-government family members may hold shares.
- If lawyer relatives take equity, a Bar Council member must first confirm that owning a company that refers work to them does not breach the rule against employing others to solicit work (Rule 136). The default is that lawyer relatives are independent suppliers, not owners.
- This is stricter than research file 03's own recommendation, which allowed the spouse to own shares with full declaration. We do not follow that. The spouse is a serving civil servant bound by r.14(2) and r.16, and "wife of the SP owns it" is the headline that option 3 warns about.
- If the officer retires, equity can be discussed fresh at that time, at fair value, with any approval the Civil Servants Act requires within two years of retirement.

**Entities.**

| Entity | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| UK Ltd (or US LLC) | Customer-facing brand, contracts, Stripe account for card payments | Stripe does not onboard Pakistani entities ([xpezia.com.pk](https://www.xpezia.com.pk/blog/7-easy-steps-for-pakistanis-to-start-a-us-company-and-unlock-stripe-paypal/)). UK is usually simpler for a services business ([research file 03, section 4.2](../research/03-regulatory-constraints.md); [globallawexperts.com](https://globallawexperts.com/incorporate-in-pakistan-vs-delaware-for-startups-2026/)). |
| Pakistan Pvt Ltd | Employs case managers and runners; invoices the UK entity for services | Pvt Ltd rather than single-member company, for a clean cap table ([globallawexperts.com](https://globallawexperts.com/how-to-register-a-company-in-pakistan-2026/)) |
| Payment rails | Stripe (UK/US), Payoneer and Wise for payouts; PayFast or Safepay for PKR-side errands | [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/resources/business/guide-to-payment-gateways-in-pakistan/); merchant-of-record fallback via Paddle |

**Tax and SBP (uncertain).** PSEB registration could give access to a 0.25% final tax on IT/ITeS exports, reported as extended to TY2029 ([ezinvoice.pk](https://ezinvoice.pk/blog/it-export-tax-rate-extended-2029-pakistan)). Whether an advisory and concierge service qualifies as IT/ITeS is **uncertain**. The same doubt applies to SBP's lighter Form R and ESFCA rules for IT exporters ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40415048/it-firms-freelancers-exempted-from-declaring-export-proceeds-of-up-to-25000); [nextgen.pk](https://nextgen.pk/news/sbp-abolishes-form-r-it-exports-esfca-dollar-retention-2026)). Do not build the model on the 0.25% rate. Confirm with PSEB, a tax adviser and the bank.

**Engagement letters and disclaimers.** Every paid engagement gets a letter that states which entity provides which service for which fee, that the company is not a law firm, and that no outcome with police, courts, NADRA or FBR is guaranteed. All information products carry "general information, not legal advice, no advocate-client relationship" ([research file 03, section 5](../research/03-regulatory-constraints.md)).

**Police and FIR matters.** Handled only by independent advocates on their own letterhead, billed directly. The company does intake, document preparation, tracking and public information. The company never contacts police about a client case.

### 5.5 Retired officers as visible advisers

Credibility should come from three things: practising-lawyer relatives (on their own letterhead, billing separately), a transparent process, and retired officials who sit on a public advisory board.

- Retired police officers, revenue officials and judges can be named, paid and visible.
- If they retired less than two years ago, they may need prior approval for private employment under the Civil Servants Act ([Pakistan Code](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/english/UY2FqaJw1-apaUY2Fqa-apaUY2NqZpY=-sg-jjjjjjjjjjjjj)). Check each one.
- Advisers review playbooks and quality. They do not call serving colleagues about client cases. That rule is in their contract.

### 5.6 What the serving officers can and cannot do

| Can (unpaid, as family) | Cannot (while serving) |
|---|---|
| Help write public-information playbooks: how an FIR works, what a challan is, which office handles what | Hold shares, directorships or options in any group entity |
| Introduce the founders to retired officers who could be advisers | Receive salary, fees, dividends, gifts or informal profit share |
| Give general family advice to the founders, as any relative would | Appear in marketing, videos, sales calls or client chats |
| Review the officer-exclusion policy itself | Be referred to as "our police contact" or by rank, in any form |
| | Speak to any police officer about a client's case |
| | Receive any client, by referral or otherwise |

The spouse follows the same column while serving.

## 6. Go-to-market and trust

### 6.1 Channels, in order

7. **Partnerships.** UK Pakistani solicitors (for Pakistan-side work they cannot do), remittance apps, RDA banks, overseas Pakistani associations. Any partner referral fee flows only on company fees.

**Caution on lawyer-fronted content.** The brief proposes explainer videos by the lawyer relatives. Competitors already acquire clients mainly through SEO blogs targeting "overseas Pakistanis plus problem" searches (research file 02, section 5). Our advantage is community referral and published prices, not out-writing them on SEO in year one.

### 7.2 Capacity

A case manager can handle about 30-40 active matters (assumption).

Scenario B has 200 retainers, 15 managed cases and 40 assessments a month across three case managers. That is more than 30-40 each if every retainer counts as an active matter. The model works only if most Watch retainers are low-touch (quarterly check, two questions), with expert time carried by the 25% retainer-delivery budget rather than case-manager time. If retainers turn out to be high-touch, Scenario B needs a fourth or fifth case manager, which reduces net by about $800-1,600 a month (assumption). Track touches per retainer from the first sign-up.

### 7.3 Tooling (no product build)

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| Client chat and intake | WhatsApp Business plus a structured web form |
| Case tracker | Airtable or Notion |
| Booking | Calendly |
| Payments | Stripe (UK/US entity) or Payoneer; Wise for payouts |
| Document vault | Google Drive, one restricted folder per client |
| Later | A client app, only after Section 9's conditions hold |

Data rules from day one, because of PECA: two-factor login on every account, per-client folder permissions, staff and supplier NDAs, no client documents on personal phones, access log reviewed monthly ([PECA](https://www.pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator6a061efe0ed5bd153fa8b79b8eb4cba7.pdf)).

### 7.4 Quality control

- **Memo templates** by category: land, inheritance, POA, tax, NADRA, company, police-related.
- **Second-eye review** of every Pathway Memo's legal content by a partner advocate before it goes out.
- **SLAs** (assumptions): question answered in 48 hours; memo in 5 working days; client update at least every 14 days on managed cases; supplier response within 2 working days.
- **Supplier scorecards**: turnaround, client score, fee adherence. An advocate who breaks the fixed fee twice is removed.
- **NPS** at closure; target 8 of 10 would recommend (assumption).

## 8. Risks and what can go wrong

Ranked by severity times likelihood (ranking is judgement).

| # | Risk | What it looks like | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Officer conflict | A client or rival alleges the business trades on a serving SP. Disciplinary or corruption case against the officer. | Full exclusion (Section 5.4) signed before launch. No mention anywhere. Police matters only via independent advocates. Written policy staff must sign. |
| 2 | Seen as a fixer or tout | Clients ask us to "make it go away". A staff member promises results. | Published scope. Scripted sales. Decline fixer requests and record them. This is a kill criterion in the test. |
| 3 | A partner advocate defrauds a client | Takes fees and disappears, or colludes with the other side | Vetting, reference checks, Bar Council registration checks. Pay-per-stage. Client pays advocate against milestones we confirm. Two-advocate bench per city. |
| 4 | Payments and FX | Stripe account frozen; PKR moves; payout delays | Two rails (Stripe plus Payoneer or Paddle). Price in USD/GBP. Keep one month of costs in foreign currency. |
| 5 | Supplier quality | Late memos, missed errands | Scorecards, SLAs, backup suppliers, second-eye review |
| 6 | Slow courts cause refund demands | Client blames us for a two-year court delay | Memo states realistic timelines in writing. We charge for navigation stages, not outcomes. Refunds only for our own missed deliverables. |
| 7 | Scope creep into litigation | Managed cases turn into open-ended hand-holding | Managed Case scoped by stage with a fixed fee. Litigation belongs to the advocate's own engagement. |
| 8 | Data leak | CNICs, FIR details or family disputes exposed | Section 7.3 controls. Minimal data retention. Breach plan. |
| 9 | A founder loses interest | Side project fades after month 4 | Written 90-day commitment. Go/no-go gate. Decide at day 90 whether each founder goes full-time. |
| 10 | Unauthorised-practice or touting complaints | A Bar Council complaint against the lawyer relatives | Wukla-style separation. No cut of legal fees. Bar Council member reviews the model before launch. |
| 11 | Reputational hit from one bad public case | A viral post about a failed case | Honest memos, documented consent, fast complaint handling, public refund policy |

## 9. Is it scalable, honestly

### 9.1 Three lenses

| Lens | Answer |
|---|---|
| Side consultancy | Yes. About $75k revenue and $38k net a year within 6-9 months is realistic if the test passes (assumption). |
| Productised company | Yes, probably. $300-650k revenue and roughly $100-260k net in 18-24 months (assumption). A very good business by Pakistan standards. A modest one by US standards. |
| Venture scale | Not by default. Pure legal and concierge services have not attracted large venture money anywhere. LawRato raised $570k; Beamcrew $0; Atrium ($75.5M) shut; UpCounsel ($26M) shut and was later revived at small scale. The diaspora companies that raised big (Zolve $251M, Vance YC, Munify YC) are fintech-first (research file 01). |

### 9.2 The venture version

If there is one, it is "the family office for the diaspora": subscription property-watch plus legal plus tax, with a fintech or property-transaction product attached later. Treat it as an option to exercise after 12 months of data. It is not the plan.

What would have to be true (all thresholds are assumptions):

| Condition | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Retainer retention | Above 85% a year |
| Customer acquisition cost | Below $100 |
| Gross margin with non-founder delivery | Above 40% |
| Attach product | A fintech or property-transaction line (for example, RDA-linked services or verified property purchase) |
| Expansion playbook | Repeatable for Bangladeshi and Indian diaspora |

### 9.3 Market size

| Step | Figure |
|---|---|
| Overseas Pakistanis | ~9 million people ([Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2330465/overbearing-overseas-pakistanis)) |
| Diaspora households | ~2 million (assumption) |
| Households with an active Pakistan-side matter | ~400,000 (assumption) |
| 1% on a retainer at $110 / month | 4,000 households, ~$5M ARR |
| 3% on a retainer at $110 / month | 12,000 households, ~$16M ARR |

A $5-16M ARR ceiling on retainers alone is a real company. It is not, alone, a venture outcome. Venture needs the attach product.

## 10. The 90-day test plan

**Rule: build no product.** Sell 20 paid cases by hand. If we cannot sell 20 cases to warm-network diaspora at real prices, no website will fix it.

### 10.1 Week by week

| Weeks | Goal | Deliverables | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (before start) | Decide the officer question | Signed exclusion letter from officer and spouse (Section 5.6) | Both founders, officer, spouse |
| 1-2 | Set up | UK Ltd or US LLC; Stripe or Paddle; WhatsApp Business; Airtable tracker; Calendly; engagement letter and disclaimer text; Bar Council member review of fee flows | Tech founder; lawyer relative for letters |
| 1-2 | Supply | 2 partner advocates (Lahore, Islamabad) signed on fixed fees and SLAs; 1 errand runner per city | Lawyer relatives, founders |
| 3-4 | Playbooks | 5 Pathway Memo templates (inheritance, POA, land occupation, non-resident tax, NICOP/attestation); 1-page price sheet in English and Urdu | Founders, advocates; officers may contribute public-information content unpaid |
| 3-4 | First sales | Message 200 warm contacts; post in 5 WhatsApp groups; 2 community-centre visits in the UK | Founders |
| 5-6 | First 10 paid cases | Deliver memos within 5 working days; collect NPS; ask for referrals | Case manager role (founders), advocates |
| 5-6 | Ambassadors | 3-5 community ambassadors on referral fee | Founders |
| 7-8 | Reach 500+ | Extend to associations and Facebook groups; offer retainers to every Case Assessment client | Founders |
| 9-10 | 20 paid cases | Hit 20; first retainer renewals; first managed case | All |
| 11-12 | Measure | Metrics against the gate below | Both founders |
| 13 | Go/no-go | Written decision | Both founders |

### 10.2 Metrics and gate (all assumptions)

**Go** if, within 60 days of the first sale:

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Paid cases | At least 20 |
| Blended revenue per paid interaction | At least $40 |
| Customers from referrals of earlier customers | At least 30% |
| Retainer sign-ups | At least 3 |
| Median time to deliver a Pathway Memo | Under 5 working days |
| Customers who would recommend | At least 8 of 10 |

**No-go** if any of these happen:

- Fewer than 10 paid cases despite reaching 500+ people in warm channels.
- Customers consistently want the problem to "go away via connections" rather than pathway or managed help. That is a fixer business and must be declined.
- Partner advocates cannot be held to fixed fees and timelines.

If the result is mixed (for example, 12-19 cases with strong NPS), run one more 30-day round with one change only, then decide (assumption).

## 11. Decision checklist for today

Answer each yes or no. Any "no" on questions 1-4 means do not start.

1. Will the serving officer hold zero equity, take zero pay, have zero role and receive zero mention in the business while serving?
2. Will the serving spouse do the same while serving?
3. Will we refuse every client who wants the problem solved through connections, and refuse to mention rank or police links in any form?
4. Will police and FIR matters be handled only by independent advocates on their own letterhead, with the company doing intake, documents, tracking and information only?
5. Will at least one practising lawyer relative review the fee flows against Bar Council rules and act as a partner advocate on their own letterhead?
6. Will the tech founder own and set up a UK or US entity with card payments?
7. Can each founder commit 10-15 hours a week for 90 days?
8. Will we accept the 90-day gate and stop if the no-go criteria are met?
9. Will we build no product until the test passes?
10. Are we content that the realistic outcome is a good small company, with venture scale as an option only?
