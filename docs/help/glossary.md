# Glossary

Plain meanings for words you will meet. The legal and government entries are general information to help you follow a conversation. They are not legal advice. If a client's situation depends on the exact rule, an independent advocate decides. Where we are unsure, we say "TODO: confirm".

## Property and land

- **Qabza.** Urdu for possession. In our work it usually means someone is illegally occupying land or a house that belongs to someone else, often while the owner is abroad.
- **Fard.** An extract from the land revenue records showing who is recorded as the owner of a piece of land. It is often the first document we ask for, because it shows what the record says today.
- **Jamabandi.** The register of land rights kept by the revenue office. A fard is copied from it.
- **Mutation (intiqal).** The entry in the land revenue records that changes the recorded owner after a sale, gift or inheritance. If mutation is not done, the record still shows the old owner, even when a deed exists.
- **Registry (sale deed).** The registered document that records a sale of property. Registering it is a separate step from mutation.
- **Illegal Dispossession Act.** A Punjab law from 2005 against forcibly taking someone's immovable property. It offers a route that is separate from an ordinary civil suit. The law and the courts that hear these cases have been changing. TODO: confirm the current position with a partner advocate before relying on this.
- **Special courts.** New courts created by federal and provincial laws in 2024 and 2025 to hear property disputes of overseas Pakistanis quickly. The federal law sets a 90-day target. In practice cases can take longer, and there is no outcome data yet.
- **Civil court.** The ordinary court for property and inheritance disputes. Slow, which is why special courts were created.
- **Stay order.** A court order that pauses an action, for example a sale, until the case is decided.

## Family and inheritance

- **Wirasat.** Inheritance. Who inherits what is set by law, and every legal heir has a share.
- **Succession certificate.** A document that confirms who the legal heirs are, so they can claim a dead person's property or accounts. In many cases NADRA issues it, but only when no heir objects. If one heir objects, the matter goes to court.
- **Power of attorney (POA).** A signed document that lets another person act for you. A general one gives wide powers and a special one covers one task. POAs are a common source of fraud, for example a relative who sells the property and keeps the money. Abroad, it is usually signed and attested at a Pakistani embassy or consulate.
- **Vakalatnama.** The signed document by which a client appoints an advocate to act for them.

## Identity and tax

- **CNIC.** Computerised National Identity Card, for people living in Pakistan.
- **NICOP.** National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis, for Pakistani citizens living abroad.
- **POC.** Pakistan Origin Card, for foreign citizens of Pakistani origin.
- **NADRA.** The National Database and Registration Authority. It issues identity cards, family records and some certificates.
- **FBR.** The Federal Board of Revenue, Pakistan's tax authority.
- **IRIS.** FBR's online system for filing returns and managing a tax profile.
- **NTN.** National Tax Number. You need one to file a return.
- **ATL.** Active Taxpayers List, the published list of people who have filed. Being on it usually means lower tax on property deals and bank transactions.
- **Filer.** A person on the ATL. A non-filer often pays higher withholding tax.
- **Withholding tax.** Tax deducted at the moment of a transaction, such as buying property or withdrawing money.
- **Roshan Digital Account (RDA).** A digital bank account that lets overseas Pakistanis invest and transact in Pakistan from abroad.

## Companies and attestation

- **SECP.** Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. It registers companies.
- **OPC (company meaning).** One Person Company, a company with a single owner.
- **OPC (commission meaning).** Overseas Pakistanis Commission, the Punjab body that takes complaints from overseas Pakistanis. Check the context to see which one is meant.
- **Attestation.** An official stamp that confirms a document is genuine so it is accepted elsewhere.
- **HEC attestation.** The Higher Education Commission verifies university degrees.
- **IBCC attestation.** The Inter Board Committee of Chairmen verifies school and college certificates and gives equivalence.
- **MOFA attestation.** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs gives the final stamp so a document can be used abroad.

## Police and fees

- **FIR.** First Information Report. A written record of a complaint about a crime, registered with the police. It starts the criminal process. At Rasta, independent advocates handle anything of substance on their own letterhead. We only do intake, document preparation, tracking and public information.
- **Challan.** A payment slip used to pay a government fee at a bank. The word is also used for the police report sent to a court. The meaning depends on context.
- **Government fees.** Court, stamp and office fees. We pass them on at cost with the receipt.

## Rasta's own words

- **Pathway Memo.** Our written plan for a problem: what it is, who owns it, the options, realistic timelines, costs and traps. It is general information, not legal advice. It is the main output of a Case Assessment.
- **Pathway Question.** A single question answered in writing within 48 hours.
- **Pathway Call.** A 30-minute call with a case manager and the relevant expert.
- **Case Assessment.** We review documents, write a Pathway Memo and walk the client through it.
- **Managed Case.** We project-manage a case with an independent advocate.
- **Errand.** A fixed-fee visit to a government office.
- **Lead.** Someone who asked for help but has not become a case yet.
- **Client.** A person we have opened a case or retainer for.
- **Case.** One piece of work for a client. Has a case number like RS-0001.
- **Intake.** The first stage, when the case is opened.
- **Triage.** Working out the category, the right office and how serious it is.
- **Stage.** Where a case is in its life: Intake, Triage, Memo drafting, Memo delivered, Managed, Errand in progress, Closed or Declined.
- **Declined.** A case we turned away, with the reason written down.
- **Next action.** The next thing to do on a case and the date to do it by. It drives the Dashboard.
- **Close out.** The last step of a case: record the client's feedback and set it to Closed.
- **Payment link.** A web address where a client can pay, for example a Stripe link.
- **Retainer.** A monthly plan (Watch, Plus or Family Office).
- **MRR.** Monthly recurring revenue. The total monthly price of active retainers.
- **Partner.** An independent professional we work with: an advocate, errand runner, adviser or accountant.
- **Partner advocate.** A lawyer who is independent of us and bills the client directly.
- **Own letterhead.** The advocate writes on their own firm's paper, not ours. It shows the legal advice is theirs.
- **SLA.** Service level agreement. Here, the number of days a partner promises to deliver in.
- **Engagement letter.** The agreement between a client and an advocate, or between a client and us.
- **Milestone plan.** The list of steps and stage fees agreed before a managed case begins.
- **Ambassador.** A community member who refers clients for a commission.
- **Referral code.** An ambassador's short code, such as UK-BHAM1. It links a lead and a case to them.
- **Commission and payout.** Commission is what we owe an ambassador. A payout is a payment of it that we have recorded.
- **Channel.** A group, page or association where we post.
- **Cadence.** How often we plan to post in a channel, in days.
- **Source.** Where a lead came from, such as website, whatsapp or ambassador.
- **NPS.** Net promoter score. A client's answer from 0 to 10 to "How likely are you to recommend us?".
- **Would recommend.** A yes or no answer to "Would you recommend us?". The share of yes answers is a gate number.
- **Gate.** The six numbers and targets that decide whether the business carries on after the 90-day test.
- **90-day test.** Selling by hand for 90 days, then deciding with the gate numbers.
- **Drive folder.** The client's Google Drive folder that holds their documents. The panel stores only the link.
- **Memo doc.** The Google Doc that holds the Pathway Memo. The panel stores only the link.
- **Acting as.** The team member name chosen in Settings. It signs your case notes.
- **Case manager.** The staff member who owns a case and is the client's contact.
- **Honeypot.** A hidden form field that only spam software fills in. A filled one means the submission is thrown away.
