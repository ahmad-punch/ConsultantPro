# What Rasta is

## The short version

Rasta (the Urdu word for "the way") helps overseas Pakistanis with problems back home. We read your situation, tell you in writing what the problem is and which office owns it, and then, if you want, we keep track of the case until it is finished.

Think of us as a guide and a project manager. We are not the lawyer and we are not the court. We make a confusing process clear, and we stay accountable while it moves.

Working name: "Rasta". The final brand name is still an open decision. See the Project screen.

## Who our customers are

People who live outside Pakistan and still carry responsibilities there. We see three groups.

- **Families in the UK with land back home.** Often several generations of family. The usual problems are inherited land, a plot someone else is occupying, a relative holding a power of attorney, or heirs who cannot agree.
- **Workers in the Gulf** (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman). The usual problems are inheritance and land, identity card renewals, document attestation, and agents who took money and stopped answering. These customers watch their budget, so they often start with a small purchase.
- **Professionals in North America and Australia.** The usual problems are tax filer status, buying property safely, registering a company and attesting degrees. This is more paperwork than dispute.

What they share: they are far away, they do not know which office to go to, and they have been burned, or know someone who has, by agents and relatives.

Three kinds of money are always kept apart:

1. **Our fee.** What is on the price list.
2. **The advocate's fee.** The customer pays the advocate directly. We take none of it.
3. **Government fees.** Paid at cost, with the official receipt.

## What we never do

- **We are not a law firm.** We do not give legal advice or legal opinions in our own name. Independent advocates give legal advice and bill the customer directly. Everything we write is "general information, not legal advice".
- **We are not a fixer.** We never offer shortcuts or speed-ups outside the official process. If a request is about getting around the process, we decline it and say why.
- **We never promise an outcome.** Nobody controls what a court or an office decides. We promise a clear plan, a fixed price and honest updates.
- **We never ask for cash** or for payment to a personal account. Customers pay by card link, Wise or Payoneer and get a receipt.
- **We never hold the customer's money, property or original documents.**
- **We never invent credibility.** No made-up reviews, names or statistics. Where we have not got real proof yet, we say so.

## How documents are handled

A customer's documents are their own. We do not keep them in our system.

- Case files live in a **Google Drive folder that the client owns**, or one we create and share with them. The client can see everything in it.
- The control panel stores only the **link** to that folder and the link to the memo document. It never stores the documents.
- We share documents only with the advocate or errand runner who needs them for the job, and only with the client's agreement.
- We never discuss a case in a group chat.
- Our data rules: two-factor login on every account, and no client documents on personal phones.
