# Metrics

## Why this screen exists

The business plan is a test, not a promise. We build no big product up front. We sell by hand for 90 days and then decide, using numbers, whether to carry on. The decision is called the gate.

This screen shows the six gate numbers, each with its target and a pass or fail mark. The plan says go if all six pass within 60 days of the first sale. If they do not, we stop or change one thing and test again.

## What you see

**The date range.** At the top, a "From" and "To" date, an "Apply" button, and four preset links: "Since first case", "First 60 days", "Last 30 days" and "This month". Everything on the screen, except channel coverage, covers only that range. By default it runs from the first case ever opened to today.

**The four supporting tables.** "Leads by source" (leads received, and how many converted). "Cases by category" (cases opened, paid, and the revenue from paid cases). "Revenue by product by month" (paid payments grouped by the month paid). "Channel coverage" (channels by platform and status, not limited by date).

**Six gate cards.** Each has a label, a big number, the target, and a small note. A green card passes. A red card fails. A grey card has no data yet. The heading shows how many of the six are passing.

### Paid cases

Target: at least 20. This counts cases opened in the date range whose payment status is Paid. A case becomes Paid only when you record a payment for it. To move this number, close sales and record every payment the day the money arrives. Open cases that never pay do not count. The Dashboard shows a similar card, but it counts all time, not a date range.

### Revenue per paid interaction

Target: at least $40. Add up every payment with status Paid, dated in the range, for both cases and retainers. Divide by how many payments that was. A low number means most sales are $15 questions. To raise it, offer the $249 Case Assessment after a question, and offer a retainer. Each monthly retainer payment counts as one paid interaction.

### Referrals

Target: at least 30 percent. On screen the card is called "Paid cases from referrals". Take the paid cases from the first card. Count those linked to an ambassador or to an earlier client's case. Divide. To raise it, set the ambassador or the "Referred by earlier case" on every case that came through someone, and ask every happy client for an introduction. If you forget to link a referral, it is not counted.

### Retainer sign-ups

Target: at least 3. This counts retainers whose start date is in the range, including ones that were later cancelled. The small note says how many are still active. To raise it, offer a retainer to every Case Assessment client and use Start retainer when they agree.

### Median days to memo

Target: under 5 working days. For each case opened in the range that has a memo delivered, count the working days (Monday to Friday) from the case being opened to the memo being marked delivered. Then take the middle value (the median). Weekends do not count. To lower it, draft memos quickly and press Mark memo delivered on the day you send the memo. If you press it late, the number gets worse. On screen the card is titled "Median working days to memo".

### Would recommend

Target: at least 80 percent, which is 8 in 10. Look at closed cases in the range where an answer was recorded at close-out. Divide the number who said yes by the number who answered. The small note also shows the average NPS score. To move it, ask the question at the end of every case and record it in Close out. Cases with "Not asked yet" are left out, so an unasked question does not help or hurt.

## Buttons and actions

### Apply

Applies the From and To dates you typed.

### Since first case

Sets the range from the day the first case was opened to today.

### First 60 days

Sets the range to the 60 days after the first case. This matches the plan's rule for the gate.

### Last 30 days

Sets the range to the last 30 days.

### This month

Sets the range from the first of this month to today.

### Save snapshot

Next to the date range. Stores the six gate values, their pass or fail, and the range on screen, with an optional note (for example "Day 60 review"). Use it at each review so you can see how the gate moved.

### History

The table under the gate cards. One row per saved snapshot, newest first: date, note, each gate value (green when it passed, red when it failed) and how many of the six passed. Archived cases are not counted in any metric.

## How it connects to other screens

It reads from almost everything: Leads (source, converted), Cases (payment status, referral links, memo date, close-out answers), Payments (amounts and dates), Retainers (start dates) and Channels (coverage). It has no forms. To change a number you change the data on the screen it came from.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking at the wrong range.** A range that starts before the first sale or ends too early will change every card. Use "First 60 days" for the official test.
- **Expecting a card to move without recording the data.** An unrecorded payment, referral link or close-out answer is invisible.
- **Reading green as "done".** The go decision is still written down in the decisions folder.
- **Treating a grey card as a failure.** Grey means no data yet.
- **Comparing with the Dashboard.** Its Paid cases is all time. This screen's is by range.
