# Clients

## Why this screen exists

A client is a person we have opened at least one case or retainer for. One person can have many cases over the years: a question today, an assessment next month, a managed project later. The Clients screen keeps the person in one record so you do not have to search every case to know who you are speaking to.

It also protects us from mistakes. When a returning client writes in, you can see what they bought before, what they paid, and whether they have a retainer, before you reply.

## What you see

**The list.** One row per client with name, contact (WhatsApp and email), location, number of cases, total paid, whether a retainer is active, and the ambassador who referred them. You can search by name, phone, email or place. Rows are in alphabetical order.

**Total paid** adds up the price of the client's cases that are marked Paid. It is not the sum of payment rows.

**A client page.** The header shows location, the date they became a client and the total paid. Below are:

- **Cases.** Every case with number, product, category, price, payment badge and stage. Click one to open it.
- **Retainers.** Each retainer with tier, monthly price, start date and status.
- **Details.** An editable form for the person's details and notes.
- **Ambassador.** Who referred them, with a link.

## Buttons and actions

### New client

Opens a form: Name, WhatsApp, Email, Country, City, Ambassador and Notes. Most of the time you will not need it, because a client is created for you when you convert a lead or open a case with "New client". Use it when you want the person on file before any work starts.

### Add client

Saves the New client form. It creates the client and opens their page.

### Save client

Saves the Details form on a client page. It overwrites the stored details.

### New case for this client

Opens the New case form with this client already chosen. Use it for repeat business so you do not create the same person twice.

### Start retainer

Opens a form: Client (fixed), Tier, Monthly (USD), Started on. Choosing a tier fills in the list price. Saving creates an active retainer. It does not take any money. Record each monthly payment under Payments.

### Cancel

Shown next to an active retainer. After a confirmation question ("Cancel this retainer?") it marks the retainer Cancelled and stamps today as the cancelled date. The history stays.

## How it connects to other screens

- **Leads.** Converting a lead creates a client, or attaches to an existing one with the same WhatsApp number or email.
- **Cases.** The client page lists the cases. A case page links back to the client.
- **Retainers.** The retainer list on this page is the same data as the Retainers screen.
- **Ambassadors.** The ambassador on a client shows who introduced them. The ambassador page lists their clients.
- **Payments.** A retainer's monthly payments are recorded on the Payments screen.

## Common mistakes

- **Creating a client twice.** Search first. If the same person appears under two records, their history is split.
- **Using New client when you meant New case.** A client with no case does not count as a sale anywhere.
- **Thinking Start retainer collects money.** It only records that the retainer exists. Record the payment separately.
- **Expecting a client to be deleted.** There is no delete button. Edit the details instead.
- **Changing a WhatsApp number carelessly.** Lead conversion matches clients by phone digits and email. A wrong number can create a duplicate next time.
- **Typing private documents into Notes.** The panel stores links and short notes only. Documents stay in the client's own Google Drive.
