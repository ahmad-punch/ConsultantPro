# Content

## Why this screen exists

Posts do not write themselves, and a plan kept in someone's head disappears when they are busy. This screen is the shared calendar for everything we publish: WhatsApp posts, group scripts, guides, videos and emails.

It answers three questions. What is planned for which week? What stage is each piece at (idea, drafted, approved, posted)? What is late? Publishing on a steady rhythm is how trust grows, and it gives the channels something to post.

## What you see

**Items grouped by week.** Each group is headed by the week it covers, for example "This week" or "Week of 20 Oct - 26 Oct", with a count. Items with no date sit in "Unscheduled" at the bottom.

**Each item shows** its title, a status badge, a red "Late" badge if it is scheduled in the past and not posted, then format, language, target platform, scheduled date and posted date. An "Open" link appears when a link is saved.

**Statuses, in order.** Idea (a thought), Drafted (the text exists), Approved (a founder has said yes), Posted (it is live).

**Formats.** Post, Script, Guide, Video, Email. **Languages.** English, Urdu, English + Urdu.

**Filters.** Search by title, and filter by status and format.

## Buttons and actions

### New item

Opens a form: Title, Format, Language, Status, Platform, Scheduled for, Link (once posted), Draft and Notes. You can paste the draft text into "Draft" so the whole piece lives in one place.

### Add item

Saves the form. If you set the status to Posted at creation, the posted date is stamped as now.

### Mark drafted

Appears on an Idea. It moves the item to Drafted. The label changes with the item: it reads "Mark approved" on a Drafted item.

### Mark approved

Appears on a Drafted item. It moves the item to Approved. Use it when a founder has read the text and agreed it can go out. Approved items are the ones the team posts.

### Mark posted

Appears on every item that is not yet Posted. It opens a small form with "Link to the post" and "Posted on". The submit button is also called Mark posted. It sets the status to Posted, sets the posted date (today by default) and saves the link if you gave one. Do it after the post is live.

### Edit

A pencil button on each row. It opens the full form to change anything, including the date. The save button is "Save". If you set the status to anything except Posted, the posted date is cleared. Setting it to Posted stamps a date if there was none.

## How it connects to other screens

- **Channels.** Content is what you post. Channels is where you post it. The two are not linked in the software. After you post an item in a group, press Mark posted here and Log post on the channel. They are two separate records.
- **Dashboard.** A quick link called "Content calendar" opens this screen.
- **Public site.** The guides on the website come from files in `content/guides/`, not from this screen. A Guide item here is only a planning note.
- **Metrics.** Content is not part of the 90-day gate.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgetting the scheduled date.** Undated items go to Unscheduled and never turn Late, so nobody chases them.
- **Marking Approved before a founder has read it.** Approval is the safety check. Everything we publish must follow the rules: no fake testimonials, no invented numbers, no promised outcomes, and the not-a-law-firm line where needed.
- **Pressing Mark posted before it is live.** The calendar then lies.
- **Assuming Mark posted also updates the channel.** It does not. Use Log post as well.
- **Leaving Urdu items unreviewed.** Urdu text needs a native review before it is approved. TODO: confirm who does this review.
- **Putting client names or case details in a draft.** Posts are public. Use general examples only.
