# Partners

## Why this screen exists

We are not a law firm. We cannot give legal advice or appear in court. Independent professionals do that work, and they bill the client directly. They are our partners.

This screen is our supplier list. The service is only as good as the people on the ground, so we keep their details, their agreed fees and their promised turnaround time in one place, and we rate them. If a partner keeps breaking the fixed fee or the deadline, the rating and the notes are the evidence for dropping them.

## What you see

**The list.** One row per partner with name, city, a note if they work on their own letterhead, type, contact, fee terms, SLA, rating, and cases. Inactive partners are faded and carry an "Inactive" badge.

**Types.**

- **Advocate.** A lawyer who advises and acts for the client, and bills the client.
- **Errand runner.** A person who visits government offices for us, for a fixed fee.
- **Adviser.** An experienced outside professional who reviews our playbooks.
- **Accountant.** A tax practitioner for filer and tax matters.

**Columns worth knowing.**

- **SLA** is the number of days the partner has agreed to deliver in. It also shows on the case page.
- **Rating** is 1 to 5 stars.
- **Cases** reads "open / total": the cases currently open with this partner, and all cases ever.
- **Own letterhead** means the advocate writes on their own firm's paper, not ours. For advocates this must be on.

**Filters.** Search by name or city. Filter by type, and by Active or Inactive.

## Buttons and actions

### New partner

Opens a form: Name, Type, City, WhatsApp, Email, SLA (days), Rating, Fee terms, and two tick boxes, "Works on own letterhead" and "Active".

Write the fee terms carefully. For advocates, record the fixed fees agreed. The advocate bills the client directly. We never collect or pass on the advocate's fee.

### Add partner

Saves the New partner form and adds the partner to the list.

### Edit

Each row has a small pencil button. Its accessible name is "Edit" followed by the partner's name. It opens the same form with the partner's details filled in.

### Save partner

Saves the edit. To stop using a partner without losing history, untick "Active" and save. They disappear from the partner choices on new cases but stay on their old cases.

## How it connects to other screens

- **Cases.** You choose a partner on the New case form or in Case details. Only active partners appear in that list. The case page shows the partner name and their SLA.
- **Dashboard.** Nothing here appears on the dashboard directly.
- **Metrics.** Partners are not in the gate, but "Median days to memo" and client feedback tell you whether a partner is slowing cases down.
- **Settings.** Our own staff are not partners. They live under Team in Settings.

## Common mistakes

- **Putting our own team here.** Case managers and operations staff belong in Settings, under Team.
- **Choosing an advocate who does not use their own letterhead for a managed case.** The business plan requires it. Check the box honestly.
- **Leaving fee terms blank.** If the advocate later changes the price, you have no record of what was agreed.
- **Deleting by habit.** There is no delete. Mark the partner inactive.
- **Rating without a reason.** A rating of 2 with no note helps nobody. Write what happened in the fee terms or in the relevant case notes.
- **Letting a partner talk to the client about our fee.** Our fee and the advocate's fee are separate. Keep the two conversations separate.
